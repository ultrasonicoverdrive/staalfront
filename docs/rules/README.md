# Game rules

Quality rule 7 (PLAN.md §5): every non-obvious game rule is documented here.
Phases 0 and 1 contain no gameplay; game rules start in phase 2 (PLAN.md §3).

| File | Rules | Work package |
|---|---|---|
| [`terrain.md`](terrain.md) | Terrain types and what they allow, the map's terrain layer, occupancy and the `Place` command. Since WP4.1 only the buildable and harvestable flags are the type's own; who crosses a type is `movement.md`. | WP2.1 |
| [`cell_overlay.md`](cell_overlay.md) | The cell grid overlay (a view, not a rule of play): the four cell states and which wins, what "occupied" means (structures only in the sim; units derived from positions), the colour data, the cost. | WP2.4 |
| [`units.md`](units.md) | Unit, structure and weapon definitions: files, every field and its unit of measure, flags, what is validated, the conversion for the sim. Stats are the design targets of `content/units.md`; the rest are placeholders. | WP3.1 |
| [`weapons.md`](weapons.md) | Weapons: every field of `weapon_defs.ron` with its unit, bounds and default (range and minimum range to the edge of the target, reload and burst in ticks, damage and damage type, the projectile kinds and their parameters, mount and firing arc), the rounding (times refused unless whole ticks, speed rounded once per tick), the reload that belongs to the unit and the burst that is dropped when it cannot go on, permanent numbers and tombstones of ids, what loading refuses, the weapon table and its checksum section (present for as long as a world has a weapon table, so earlier checksums stay), the two-pass tick of shooting (decide everything, then apply the damage summed per victim), the report-only `hp_after`, what WP5.4 filled in, the shipped set, the `weapons_reload` scenario. | WP5.1, WP5.2, WP5.3 |
| [`projectiles.md`](projectiles.md) | Projectiles as separate entities: the four flights (straight toward where the target stood, arcing and landing, guided with a turn limit and the last known point, falling), when and why a projectile goes off (proximity, arrival, fuse), what a moving or dying target means for each kind, the shooter that dies with a shell in the air, the hook WP5.3 filled in, order independence, the store and its checksum section, the snapshot, the tuned rocket, and whether `projectile_speed` is required. | WP5.2 |
| [`armour.md`](armour.md) | Armor and splash: the five armor types, the damage-times-armor table in percent (complete or refused, optional for the frozen copies), how a hit is rounded (once, to the nearest, a tie up, at least one hit point unless the percentage is zero), splash with a full-strength radius and a straight falloff measured from the edge of a body, the decisions (the direct victim is hurt once; friendly fire is a flag of the weapon, on by default; the shooter is no exception), the radius query in the crowd pass's spatial index, order independence, the digest and what was re-pinned, the shipped placeholder table, the `armour_splash` scenario. | WP5.3 |
| [`targeting.md`](targeting.md) | Target selection and return fire: opt-in per world (`with_targeting`, two numbers in `sim.ron`), a chosen target as an order the unit gives itself (`Pursue`) with the old order begun again afterwards, what each stance does (idle, hold, guard, attack-move; a move never stops), candidates and the score (the armor table's percentage times damage and rate, so a rifle prefers infantry and a rocket vehicles; a weapon that does nothing never picks) and the total key (score, distance, position, only then slot), the scan cooldown, when a target is dropped, return fire in reach or followed within a pursuit distance, the range check and the duplicated range (`weapon_range` stays, nothing new reads it; a hull weapon on a turret unit is refused), the simultaneity rules and the spawn-order tests, units at zero hit points, the checksum (section `A`, no pinned value moved), the `tanks_vs_infantry` scenario with its measured numbers. | WP5.4 |
| [`destruction.md`](destruction.md) | Destruction: opt-in per world (`with_destruction`, three numbers in `sim.ron`), death at one defined point of the tick (after all the damage is applied, before the return fire; a mutual kill kills both), what removal does (the generation goes up, orders, engagements, turrets, guards and projectiles aimed at the dead end cleanly), zero hit points no longer meaning two things, the cells freed and the routes rebuilt, wrecks (vehicles by body size) and rubble (by footprint) that block nothing, expire after a number of ticks, are sorted by place and are cleared by a building, kill credit settled from the damage of the killing tick (enemy damage only; most damage wins, ties by place, never by slot or `hp_after`; assists; tallies per player and kills per unit), the effects hook (`Snapshot::deaths`), the checksum section `X`, the permutation tests, what is deferred (death blasts, veterancy, accumulated credit), and the game: weapons, targeting and destruction in the sandbox, sides placed out of reach, wrecks drawn from the models (vehicle wrecks of set v3, rubble of set v4; files, size bounds and colour in `wrecks.ron`). | WP5.5 |
| [`effects.md`](effects.md) | Visual effects: the lossless effects event queue (recorded after every sim step, capped at 4096 with a drop counter), `effects.ron` (bursts, links by damage type and weapon id, deaths, wreck smoke, tracers, limits, every number pinned), the audio event names, the one particle pool and the one instanced premultiplied-alpha draw call (counted in `DrawStats`, no per-frame allocation, drawn after the icons), the muzzle anchors of the glb loader (`muzzle*` and `fx_*` nodes kept, re-parented when their part is pruned, posed like the drawn unit, a burst alternating between the muzzles, wrecks told from units by slot) and the bounding-box fallback, the zoom rules (pixel clamp, fade with the icon cross-fade, half the particles at middle zoom, a death flash at strategic zoom), wreck smoke and projectile tracers, the combat benchmark `sf_demo --bench --fight` and the pinned checksums of its headless twin (and why the plain benchmark no longer ends at M0's value), the tests and mutations, the relative cost, what is not done. | WP5.6a, WP5.6b |
| [`audio.md`](audio.md) | Positional sound: the one distance, zoom and pan model (`spatial::mix`, every number in `audio.ron`), how a shown effect event becomes a sound (name, bank or mapping, silent when there is none), the planner's rules per frame (quiet, order by priority then loudness, merge within a tick and radius, voice limit, spacing that grows with distance and zoom), the tests, the gaps. | WP11.3 |
| [`economy.md`](economy.md) | Ore on the map: field cells (the harvestable terrain), the amount per cell and its numbers in `ore.ron` (start, maximum, regrowth amount and period), taking (`Ore::take`, `Command::TakeOre`), the decision that an emptied cell stays an `ore` cell and grows back, the staggered regrowth that touches `cells / period` cells per tick, the nearest-ore search with its tie order, the checksum section `E` (only with ore rules, a running sum), the fill levels of the look and the minimap, measured cost, who uses it. Since WP7.2 also the harvesters: the loop (search, mine, drive back, check in, unload), the numbers of `harvest.ron`, claims on ore cells, the messages between harvester and refinery (request, grant, queue, arrived, cancel, revoked, unloaded, released), one bay with a first-come queue and a grant timeout, the two-pass tick of harvesting, the cases (no ore, no refinery, a refinery or a harvester destroyed, several refineries, a player order to a field, a harvester stuck in traffic), the delivered-ore counter that WP7.3 and WP7.4 replace, the decision about `Command::TakeOre`, the checksum section `H`, the cargo bar and the measured cost of the minimap upload and of the stage. Since WP7.3 also storage: the capacity per building (`storage` in the building block, the refinery 2000), the capacity of a player as the sum over standing structures, the bite cut to the room, nothing clamped when capacity shrinks, the checksum section `V`, the storage bar on the refinery and the stored / capacity readout. Money is to follow. | WP7.1, WP7.2, WP7.3 |
| [`buildings.md`](buildings.md) | Building definitions: the optional `building` block of a Structure record (function enum, the exit side units use, prerequisites), where footprint, build time, price, power and faction availability already live, every refusal at load (unknown prerequisite, cycle, footprint 0 or too big, negative build time, a factory without an exit side, the banned names, duplicate or reused ids), the frozen copies that load without a block, the simulation's `BuildingTable` and its checksum section `B` (only with a table, so no earlier pin moved), the shipped placeholder tech tree per faction as a diagram and a table, what it is not (the campaign unlock order, unit prerequisites). | WP6.1 |
| [`construction.md`](construction.md) | Construction: a structure built with `BuildStructure` goes up for its `build_ticks` (one integer of state per entity), counts as a prerequisite and a base anchor only when complete (`World::stands`, for power and production later), the order inside a tick (the state first, then the commands, so the picture follows the state and a mission event fires from `World::completions()`, never from an animation), a structure at zero hit points does not finish, the checksum section `N` (only while something is going up), why `placement.expected` moved and what was measured, the stage models and progress bar from `build_stages.ron` with a fallback box, the gaps (no cost, no cancel, full hit points). | WP6.3 |
| [`orders.md`](orders.md) | Orders as unit state: stand still (idle), move, attack, attack-move, guard, stop and scatter, the shift queue, arrival tolerance, destination versus route, what phases 4 and 5 replace, command sequence numbers. | WP3.4 |
| [`icons.md`](icons.md) | Strategic icons: the format for artists (64 x 64 PNG, white on transparent, black or cut-out sign), names and `icons.ron`, how a role gets its icon and what the fallback is, how the engine draws it (tint, rim, outline, UI scale), the 14 px distinguishability rule, how to replace a placeholder. | WP3.6 |
| [`cursor.md`](cursor.md) | The cursor context: which cursor the mouse shows (select, inspect, move, queue, attack, attack-move, guard, no-go, eight scroll arrows, placeholders for later phases) and so which order a right-click gives, the decision table, what each movement class can enter, the picture format with hotspots, how the software cursor is drawn and when the system cursor is hidden. | WP3.5 |
| [`movement.md`](movement.md) | Movement classes and terrain costs: the table (foot, wheeled, tracked, hover, water, air against grass, road, water, rock, ore, bridge), where it lives and how it is validated, the bridge rule (land crosses, vessels pass under), how a cost scales a step, a unit stopping at the edge with its order kept, the corner and snap rules, the spawn rule, what ignores terrain, why slope waits, the checksum, the `terrain_costs` scenario. | WP4.1 |
| [`pathfinding.md`](pathfinding.md) | Hierarchical A* for single units: which classes are routed, the costs, sectors of 16 by 16 cells and their portals, the abstract graph and how a sector is rebuilt when a structure changes, the stages of a request, the thick line of sight, the unreachable-goal rule, the route a unit keeps (ring, portal path, refill), the node budget and the queue in the order of the ids, the checksum section; flowfields for groups (WP4.3); replanning (WP4.7): what makes a unit ask again, the overlay of blocked cells for one request, giving an order up, a goal built over, the round robin of the looks. | WP4.2, WP4.3, WP4.7 |
| [`collision.md`](collision.md) | Collision and local avoidance: the body (half the `size`, softer for infantry), the crowd pass (spatial grid, passes, who gives way: movers by mass, an idle friend a little, a holder never), following, keeping right, wait then detour, a stuck unit stepping to a free neighbour and asking for a new route round the bodies that stand still and giving the order up after a few tries (WP4.7), order independence, arrival slots made for bodies and arrival in a crowd, why five cells cannot hold 200 bodies, the numbers, `bridge_200` and `crowd_jam` measured. | WP4.4 |
| [`turning.md`](turning.md) | Turning: how the hull turns before it drives per movement class (infantry at once, tracked on the spot, wheeled, hover and water in arcs of a smallest radius with a turn on the spot inside the circle), the shortest way and the tie at half a turn, how avoidance and path following meet the turn constraint (pushes bypass it, driven steps do not), the turret with a facing of its own (target, attack order, hull; `AimTurret`; interpolated for the renderer; the barrel's pitch as a hook), the data, the checksum section, the scenarios that moved and `bridge_200` measured, the `turning` scenario. | WP4.5 |
| [`formations.md`](formations.md) | Formations: who forms one (a move order for 3 to 19 units that cross the same ground; the decision that this is the default and why a crowd of 200 is not one), the lattice made from the group's layout, the leader and the slots that turn with the heading, the pace of the slowest, what a member does (lead, walk to its slot, follow the order's way, wait), narrow places (loose) and re-forming, the end and the turned arrival slots, the numbers of `sim.ron`, the checksum section, `formation_move` measured. | WP4.6 |
| [`selection.md`](selection.md) | Selection as presentation state: click, drag rectangle, shift, double-click, control groups, inspecting an enemy, the dead leaving the selection, the feedback (rings, icons, health bars, drag rectangle). | WP3.3 |
