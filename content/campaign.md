# Campaigns

Ten missions per faction (PLAN.md phase 15). M2 needs missions 1–3 of each faction
with briefings.

- **Type**: one or two of the mission types from `docs/mission-inspiration.md` §4:
  destroy base, rescue/escort, commando, convoy, defend until time runs out,
  infiltration, bridge/crossing, time pressure, escape, conquest, fakes and deception.
- **Teaches**: the new thing the player learns or gets.
- **Twist**: what changes halfway through.
- **Story beat**: what this mission means for the larger story.

## Checks

- [ ] No more than 3 "destroy base" missions per faction.
- [ ] At least one mission without a base (commando or escort) per faction.
- [ ] Every new unit or building is introduced in a mission that teaches it.
- [ ] The difficulty rises, with an easier mission after each hard one.
