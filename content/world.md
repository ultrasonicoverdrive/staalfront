# World bible

Fill this in before any mission. It decides the art direction, the look of the factions
and the tone of every briefing.

## 1. Setting in one paragraph

_Where and when, what went wrong, why there is a war. 3–6 sentences._

## 2. Tone

_Serious / over-the-top / satirical / grim. Name a few reference works for the tone only
(film, comics). Not for content._

## 4. Places

_Regions or fronts where the campaigns take place, in a few words each. Every one of
them becomes a terrain look and a briefing background._

## 5. Technology level

_What exists and what doesn't: helicopters, aircraft, ships, superweapon(s), walls,
mines? This decides which models and icons need to be made._

## 6. Things we never do

_Lines you don't want to cross: real nations, real people, real atrocities, and so on._
