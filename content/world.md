# World bible — Staalfront

## 1. Setting
In an alternate late-industrial era, two neighbouring powers depend on the same railways, hydroelectric dams, fuel depots, and radio networks. A series of precise attacks triggers mobilisation on both sides, while forged orders and missing supply records make each government believe the other struck first. The conflict spreads because neither side can trust its communications. As the campaigns progress, field commanders uncover evidence that a private logistics-and-intelligence network is deliberately escalating the war.

## 2. Tone
Grounded, tense, and occasionally dryly ironic. The world should feel like a classic isometric RTS: clear silhouettes, bold faction identities, short briefings, memorable tactical twists, and a serious core beneath the pulp-military presentation. Avoid direct imitation of any one game's dialogue, characters, scenes, or distinctive mission concepts.

## 3. Factions

| | Meridian Compact | Veyran Directorate |
|---|---|---|
| What they want | Protect transport routes and stop attacks on civilian infrastructure | Prevent encirclement and secure the border |
| Why the player might like them | Resourceful, cooperative, improvisational | Disciplined, resilient, technically capable |
| Shapes | Mixed equipment, practical field repairs, rounded utility vehicles | Angular silhouettes, standardised armour, heavy riveted structures |
| Colours besides player colour | Sand, olive, weathered blue | Slate, charcoal, muted crimson |
| Emblem idea | Three linked road lines around a small sun | A vertical tower above three horizontal bars |
| Strength | Logistics, field repair, flexible task forces | Infantry discipline, defensive positions, strong artillery |
| Weakness | Uneven supply and slower coordination | Rigid doctrine and vulnerability to false intelligence |
| Exclusive unit roles | field_repair, scout_team, light_transport | combat_engineer, heavy_gun_team, fortified_bunker |

## 4. Places
- **North Ridge:** cold farmland, rail junctions, low hills.
- **The Vale:** wooded roads, bridges, villages, and a vulnerable dam.
- **Coastworks:** pumping stations, docks, storage tanks, and industrial canals.
- **Glass Orchard:** abandoned agricultural research buildings and irrigation channels.
- **Black Basin:** foundries, command relays, and the final coordination hub.

## 5. Technology level
Late-industrial alternate history. Conventional infantry, tanks, artillery, trucks, engineers, radar, limited aircraft and coastal craft are possible. No magic, aliens, or supernatural technology. Advanced automated defence exists only as a late-campaign prototype and must be foreshadowed.

## 6. Things we never do
No real-world nations or real political leaders. No copied Red Alert characters, dialogue, mission titles, named weapons, cutscenes, or plot twists. No glorification of atrocities. Civilians are people to protect, not disposable set dressing.
