# Cast

## CAPTAIN ELIAN
- Faction: Meridian Compact
- Role in the story: Field commander whose decisions anchor Faction 1's campaign.
- Appears in missions: F1-M01–F1-M10
- Look: late 30s, lean, tired eyes, practical field jacket, folded map in breast pocket.
- Poses/emotions needed: neutral, pointing at map, determined, angry, quietly relieved.
- Voice: mid-low, measured, northern European accent in English, controlled.
- Speech habit or catchphrase: gives orders in short verbs; never exaggerates.
- Notes: Never a superhero; earns trust through clarity.

## MIRA
- Faction: Meridian Compact
- Role in the story: Intelligence and communications officer who spots inconsistencies.
- Appears in missions: F1-M01–F1-M10
- Look: early 30s, close-cropped hair, headset, field tablet and pencil.
- Poses/emotions needed: analytical, concerned, pointing at map, startled, resolute.
- Voice: mid, precise, quick when stressed, dry.
- Speech habit or catchphrase: asks what the evidence actually proves.
- Notes: Often identifies the twist but does not solve the whole mystery alone.
