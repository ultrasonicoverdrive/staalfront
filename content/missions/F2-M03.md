## B. Story

- **Situation**: A ferry crew and a group of displaced families are trapped on the far side of a lake. Open a corridor and escort them to the evacuation point.
- **Briefing**: `briefings/F2-M03.fountain`
- **Mid-mission twist**: The ferry's engine fails; a repair team must reach it while infantry hold the shoreline.
- **Story beat**: The faction's public purpose is tested by the cost of the conflict.

- **Ending (win)**: The families reach the evacuation camp. Among the evacuees, Lenka hears the same story from people from both sides of the border: identical orders, with each side's seal, telling villages to expect an attack. The Directorate's stated purpose — protecting its people — has been met at the cost of a shoreline, and the evacuees' accounts become the Passenger List interlude.
- **Ending (loss)**: The ferry goes down in the shallows, or too many families die on the bank. The surviving escort withdraws. The Compact holds the east shore, the lake stays closed, and the Directorate's first public rescue of the war is a funeral.

## C. Map

- **Map**: new
- **Size and terrain**: Large, 96×96 cells; a long lake across the north-centre, ferry landing on the south-east shore, evacuation camp in the south-west, enemy road from the east edge. Marsh (slow) along the east shore, pine on the east road.
- **Sketch**: create `missions/F2-M03-map.png` during map production.
- **Sketch description**: The lake fills the north third of the map from the west edge to a wooded headland in the north-east. A stone pier (P_FAR_PIER) on the north shore is cut off from the player by the lake and by cliffs on the north edge; the ferry waits there. A charted shoal bar (Z_SHOAL) lies in the lake between the pier and the landing, so the ferry cannot sail straight: it keeps to the deep channel west of the bar, via two route points, and comes back east to the stall position (about 47 cells instead of 25). The south-east shore has a timber landing (P_FERRY_LANDING) with a shallow-water apron of 4 cells in front of it. Shallows (walkable, slow) run 6 cells out from the landing; the ferry stalls at the edge of these shallows. A marsh road runs from the landing south-west to the player's forward base in the south-centre, then west to the evacuation camp in the south-west corner (tents, sandbags, a neutral flag). The enemy road enters at the east edge through pines and reaches the landing from the east. A second enemy track comes down from the north-east headland along the shore.

**Win**: all 8 civilians accounted for (evacuated + dead = 8) with at most 3 dead (TR_14).

## J. Flow in words

1. 0:00 — Briefing. Player sees the forward base, the camp, and the ferry waiting across the lake. 1200 credits, one truck, one engineer, 8 riflemen, 2 rocket troopers.
2. 0:30–2:30 — Player decides what to spend on: a second transport_truck (600) is the obvious buy; an extra engineer (500) is the careful one. Harvester starts income.
3. 2:30–5:00 — March to the shoreline. The picket (5 riflemen, scout, light tank) holds the marsh around the landing. Rocket troopers for the tank, riflemen for the rest. Marsh slows everyone.
4. ~5:00 — Shoreline clear. Dravec: ferry casting off, four minutes across. Player sends the engineer forward and sets up on the landing.
5. ~9:00 — Twist: the ferry stops 6 cells out. Three-minute hold timer on screen. First assault (6 riflemen, 1 tank) comes up the east road. The tank will shoot the ferry if it reaches the shore.
6. 9:00–10:00 — Engineer wades into the shallows to the ferry (7 cells at half speed, about 20 s from the landing). Boarding repairs the engine; ferry resumes toward the landing (7 cells at 0.2 cells/s, 35 s).
7. ~10:00 — With prompt engineer boarding, the ferry docks and 8 civilians appear on the landing. Player loads 5 into the first truck. Docking time depends on when the engineer boards, not on a fixed mission timestamp.
8. 10:30 in this example (90 s after the stall) — Second assault from the headland shore track (4 riflemen, 2 tanks), even if the ferry has already docked. The force threatens the landing and evacuation road; tanks target the ferry only while it remains a valid target.
9. ~11:30 in this example (90 s after docking) — Pursuit team enters the east road and chases the trucks along the marsh road. Riflemen left on the landing or at the base cover the route.
10. 10:00–14:00 — Two truck runs (or two trucks once) to the camp; a run is about 47 s each way (83 cells, the first 20 in marsh), so about 2:00 for the round trip with loading. Each civilian unloaded inside Z_EVACUATION counts.
11. ~14:30 — Eighth civilian accounted for with at most 3 lost. Dravec's line; win.

  1. TR_01 fires once; ferry visible at P_FAR_PIER from the start; objectives A and D visible.
  2. Kill the picket but keep all player units outside Z_SHORELINE: TR_02 does not fire. Move one unit in: TR_02 fires, ferry departs, `timer_crossing` starts.

  9. Kill the only engineer with the ferry stalled while the barracks stands: no loss; train a new engineer and repair. Then also lose the barracks: lose.

## Notes

All content is original: no characters, dialogue, missions or scenes are taken from existing games. The mission structure follows common genre patterns (base-building, commando, escort, timed defence, sabotage).

**Timing check** (speeds from `units.md`; distance in cells ÷ speed = time; retuned 17-10-2026, Tijmen approved):
- Ferry (0.2 cells/s): P_FAR_PIER (69, 6) → P_FERRY_ROUTE_1 (57, 14) 14.4 cells → P_FERRY_ROUTE_2 (50, 23) 11.4 cells → P_FERRY_STALL (70, 31) 21.5 cells = 47.4 cells ÷ 0.2 = 237 s, so TR_04 fires at 3:57 (`timer_crossing` 240 s is the backstop) and the stall happens at about 9:00 in the flow, the "four minutes across" of MSG_03. The straight line P_FAR_PIER → P_FERRY_STALL is 25 cells = 125 s, which would have fired TR_04 two minutes early; Z_SHOAL blocks it. The old `timer_crossing` was 120 s, which matched neither the dialogue nor the old ferry speed (62 s). The last 7 cells to the landing take 35 s.
- Wading: P_FERRY_LANDING (70, 38) → P_FERRY_STALL (70, 31) 7 cells at half speed (0.35) = 20 s.
- Truck run: P_FERRY_LANDING → P_EVAC_CAMP (11, 85) about 83 cells = 20 cells of marsh at 1.1 (18 s) + 63 cells at 2.2 (29 s) = 47 s one way. Civilians on foot (0.6) need about 2:20 without the marsh.
- T_PURSUIT: P_EAST_ENTRY (94, 44) → landing 24 cells: tank 12 s, riflemen 34 s; released 90 s after docking, so it reaches the landing while the second run is loading.
- `timer_hold` is a 180 s assault window measured from the stall, not a mandatory repair duration. TR_06 releases the second assault 90 s into that window, including after early docking; MSG_05 reports that assault rather than implying that docking must wait. The pursuit timer starts at actual docking. All times in J are illustrative; event-relative triggers govern play. Target length remains 16 minutes.
