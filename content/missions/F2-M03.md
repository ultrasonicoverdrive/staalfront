## B. Story

- **Situation**: A ferry crew and a group of displaced families are trapped on the far side of a lake. Open a corridor and escort them to the evacuation point.
- **Briefing**: `briefings/F2-M03.fountain`
- **Mid-mission twist**: The ferry's engine fails; a repair team must reach it while infantry hold the shoreline.
- **Story beat**: The faction's public purpose is tested by the cost of the conflict.

- **Ending (win)**: The families reach the evacuation camp. Among the evacuees, Lenka hears the same story from people from both sides of the border: identical orders, with each side's seal, telling villages to expect an attack. The Directorate's stated purpose — protecting its people — has been met at the cost of a shoreline, and the evacuees' accounts become the Passenger List interlude.
- **Ending (loss)**: The ferry goes down in the shallows, or too many families die on the bank. The surviving escort withdraws. The Compact holds the east shore, the lake stays closed, and the Directorate's first public rescue of the war is a funeral.

## C. Map

- **Map**: new
- **Size and terrain**: Large, 96×96 cells; a long lake across the north-centre, ferry landing on the south-east shore, evacuation camp in the south-west, enemy road from the east edge. Marsh (slow) along the east shore, pine on the east road.
- **Sketch**: create `missions/F2-M03-map.png` during map production.
- **Sketch description**: The lake fills the north third of the map from the west edge to a wooded headland in the north-east. A stone pier (P_FAR_PIER) on the north shore is cut off from the player by the lake and by cliffs on the north edge; the ferry waits there. The south-east shore has a timber landing (P_FERRY_LANDING) with a shallow-water apron of 4 cells in front of it. Shallows (walkable, slow) run 6 cells out from the landing; the ferry stalls at the edge of these shallows. A marsh road runs from the landing south-west to the player's forward base in the south-centre, then west to the evacuation camp in the south-west corner (tents, sandbags, a neutral flag). The enemy road enters at the east edge through pines and reaches the landing from the east. A second enemy track comes down from the north-east headland along the shore.

## I. Objectives (as the player sees them)

| ID | Text (English) | Required? | Completed by trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Clear the Compact picket from the ferry landing shoreline. | yes | TR_02 |
| B | Hold the shoreline until the ferry docks. | yes | TR_07 (the docking completes it together with C) |
| C | Get an engineer onto the stalled ferry and repair the engine. | yes | TR_07 |
| D | Bring the families to the evacuation camp. No more than 3 may die. | yes | TR_14 |

**Win**: all 8 civilians accounted for (evacuated + dead = 8) with at most 3 dead (TR_14).
**Lose**: 4 or more civilians dead (TR_12); OBJ_FERRY destroyed before docking (TR_13); yard destroyed with no infantry and no barracks (TR_15); no engineer and no way to train one while the ferry is stalled (TR_16).

## J. Flow in words

1. 0:00 — Briefing. Player sees the forward base, the camp, and the ferry waiting across the lake. 1200 credits, one truck, one engineer, 8 riflemen, 2 rocket troopers.
2. 0:30–2:30 — Player decides what to spend on: a second transport_truck (600) is the obvious buy; an extra engineer (500) is the careful one. Harvester starts income.
3. 2:30–5:00 — March to the shoreline. The picket (5 riflemen, scout, light tank) holds the marsh around the landing. Rocket troopers for the tank, riflemen for the rest. Marsh slows everyone.
4. ~5:00 — Shoreline clear. Petrov: ferry casting off, four minutes across. Player sends the engineer forward and sets up on the landing.
5. ~7:00 — Twist: the ferry stops 6 cells out. Three-minute hold timer on screen. First assault (6 riflemen, 1 tank) comes up the east road. The tank will shoot the ferry if it reaches the shore.
6. 7:00–8:30 — Engineer wades into the shallows to the ferry (about 25 s from the landing). Boarding repairs the engine; ferry resumes toward the landing.
7. 8:30 — Second assault from the headland shore track (4 riflemen, 2 tanks). Ninety-second warning. This is the hard moment: two tanks, two rocket troopers, maybe a third if the player built one.
8. ~9:30 — Ferry docks, 8 civilians appear on the landing. Player loads 5 into the first truck. The civilians walk at speed 3 and die to anything; the trucks are the point of the mission.
9. 11:00 — Pursuit team enters the east road and chases the trucks along the marsh road. Riflemen left on the landing or at the base cover the route.
10. 11:00–15:00 — Two truck runs (or two trucks once) to the camp. Each civilian unloaded inside Z_EVACUATION counts.
11. ~15:30 — Eighth civilian accounted for with at most 3 lost. Petrov's line; win.

## K. After the mission

- **Carry over**: Campaign flag `completed_F2-M03`; `civilians_dead` carries as `ferry_losses_F2-M03` (number) and is quoted in the Passenger List interlude. Evidence flag `evidence_F2-M03` is set on win with `civilians_dead` ≤ 1 (the evacuees trust the escort enough to hand over the forged village orders). No unit carry-over.
- **Choice**: Continue to F2-M04.
- **Test scenario**:
  1. TR_01 fires once; ferry visible at P_FAR_PIER from the start; objectives A and D visible.
  2. Kill the picket but keep all player units outside Z_SHORELINE: TR_02 does not fire. Move one unit in: TR_02 fires, ferry departs, `timer_crossing` starts.
  3. Ferry stops at P_FERRY_STALL at 2:00 after departure; MSG_04, 3:00 hold timer shown, T_STALL_ASSAULT_1 spawns at the east edge.
  4. Engineer reaches the ferry: it resumes and docks; 8 civilians appear on the landing; the engineer reappears on the landing.
  5. Let the hold timer expire with the ferry still stalled: no loss; the ferry stays; a late engineer can still repair it.
  6. Destroy the ferry (enemy tank fire) before docking: MSG_08, lose.
  7. Kill 3 civilians: no loss. Kill a 4th: lose.
  8. Unload 5 civilians inside Z_EVACUATION: `civilians_evacuated` = 5, they vanish into the camp. Deliver the remaining 3: win.
  9. Kill the only engineer with the ferry stalled while the barracks stands: no loss; train a new engineer and repair. Then also lose the barracks: lose.
  10. T_PURSUIT spawns 90 s after docking and targets trucks first.

## Notes

This is an original real-time-strategy campaign inspired by the broad structure of classic 1990s RTS campaigns: faction-specific operations, base-building, commando missions, escorts, timed defence, sabotage, and escalating counterattacks. It does not reuse Red Alert characters, dialogue, mission scripts, or named story events.
