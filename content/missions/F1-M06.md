## B. Story

- **Situation**: A damaged hydroelectric dam threatens to flood the valley. Evacuate the engineers and withdraw the garrison before the access road disappears.
- **Briefing**: `briefings/F1-M06.fountain`
- **Mid-mission twist**: The evacuation route is cut; a secondary route opens through a half-submerged industrial yard.
- **Story beat**: The campaign shifts from holding territory to preventing a wider catastrophe.

| MSG_07 | CAPTAIN ELIAN | "That is everyone we could reach. Mira, Venn's notes on the breach go to command tonight." |

- **Ending (win)**: Venn and the surviving workers are on the ridge when the crest gives way and the valley floor disappears under grey water. Venn's first report is short: the breach charges were placed at the one point on the dam that would flood the valley and spare the eastern bank. Holding ground is no longer the question. Preventing the next breach is.
- **Ending (loss)**: The crest goes before the ridge is reached. The valley road, the garrison and the control house are under water, and the one person who could explain how the dam was broken is lost with them. Command orders the remaining units back over the pass with no answers.

## C. Map

- **Map**: new
- **Size and terrain**: 96×96 cells. The Vale. A concrete dam runs along the north edge (rows 4–10, columns 30–78). The river leaves the dam at column 62–70 and runs south along the eastern third of the map. Everything east of the river is the enemy's bank. The valley floor (west of the river, south of the dam) is farmland and a single metalled road. A wooded ridge rises in the south-west corner.

Left of the road, between the control buildings and the hill, a walled pumping yard with tanks and rusted gantries, its gate shut. On the east bank: an enemy field camp and a track up onto the dam crest.

## I. Objectives (as the player sees them)

| ID | Text (English) | Required? | Completed by trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Reach ENGINEER VENN at the control house and bring him to the high ground. | yes | TR_13 |
| B | Evacuate at least 5 of the 8 workers from the village to the high ground. | yes | TR_12 |
| C | Recover the charge fragment from the inspection gallery on the dam crest. | no | TR_14 |

**Win**: Objectives A and B are complete, and either no player mobile unit remains outside Z_HIGH_GROUND or `timer_main` has expired.
**Lose**: OBJ_VENN is destroyed; or 4 or more civilians are killed; or `timer_main` expires with objective A or B incomplete; or the player has no mobile units left while Venn is not yet on the high ground.

## J. Flow in words

1. **0:00** Briefing. Player holds a small garrison on the valley floor with 2 amphibious_transport and a light_transport. Timer 17:00 visible. Markers on the control house (north) and the village (north-east).
2. **0:10** Venn radios that enemy riflemen are holding the control house. Player sends tanks and infantry north up the low road, and transports to the village.
3. **2:00** T_RAID_1 crosses the stone bridge to reinforce the control house. The player must break it to free Venn (TR_07). Meanwhile transports load civilians (4 per light_transport, 5 per amphibious_transport) and run them to the ridge.
4. **3:00–8:00** T_HARASS every 120 s and T_RAID_2 at 5:30 press the village and the road. Village must be empty before 8:00.
5. **8:00** Stage 1: river, bridge and village flood. The road dip at P_ROAD_DIP becomes shallow; tanks north of it can only come south inside an amphibious_transport (one vehicle per trip). The eastern bank is now cut off.
6. **8:30** MSG_06 warning.
7. **9:30** T_CREST comes over the dam walkway and down into the control house area, pushing down the road. If Venn is still north of the dip, this is the crisis.
8. **10:00–13:00** Player ferries remaining units over the dip, optionally sends a scout or rifleman up the crest ramp for the charge fragment (objective C), and abandons the garrison.
9. **13:00** Stage 2: low road, garrison, control house and crest flood. Anything still there is lost. The yard gate breaks and the pumping yard becomes a shallow-water route; only infantry (slow) and amphibious_transport can cross it.
10. **13:00–17:00** Stragglers in the north cross the yard to P_YARD_EXIT and climb the ridge. Player can build extra amphibious_transport only if the vehicle_factory was still standing before 13:00, so forward planning matters.
11. **~16:00** Win when Venn and ≥5 civilians are on the ridge and nothing is left outside, or at 17:00 with the same conditions. `units_saved` recorded.
12. **End** Venn's report: the breach was deliberate, placed to spare the eastern bank. Leads into the "Waterline" interlude.

## K. After the mission

- **Carry over**: Campaign flag `completed_F1-M06`; `units_saved` (shown in the next briefing as "garrison strength"); flag `m06_tech_crew_saved` if all 3 of OBJ_TECH_CREW reach Z_HIGH_GROUND. Surviving units do not carry over as units.
- **Evidence**: `evidence_m06` (charge fragment). If set, the "Waterline" interlude and the F1-M07 briefing add a line that the charge casing matches neither faction's issue.
- **Choice**: Continue to F1-M07. Evidence changes briefing text only.
- **Test scenario**:
  1. TR_01 fires once; all three timers run from 0:00 and `timer_main` shows 17:00.
  2. OBJ_VENN cannot be selected or moved until TR_07; TR_07 does not fire while any Enemy 1 unit is in Z_DAM_WORKS.
  3. At 8:00 a civilian left in Z_WORKER_VILLAGE dies and `civilians_lost` increments; a light_tank at P_ROAD_DIP after 8:00 cannot path south, an amphibious_transport carrying it can.
  4. At 13:00 OBJ_YARD_GATE is removed, Z_SUBMERGED_YARD is pathable for infantry and amphibious_transport and not for light_tank; player structures in Z_GARRISON are destroyed; MSG_05 plays exactly once.
  5. With Venn on the ridge and only 4 civilians safe, TR_15 does not fire; the 5th civilian entering Z_HIGH_GROUND completes B and then TR_15 fires if nothing is outside.
  6. Killing OBJ_VENN at any time triggers TR_16 and no other ending; `timer_main` expiry with A complete and B incomplete triggers TR_18 only.
  7. Picking up OBJ_CHARGE_FRAGMENT sets `evidence_m06`; the crate is gone after 13:00 if not collected (crest flooded).
  8. T_HARASS does not spawn after TR_06; T_CREST still arrives at 9:30 across the crest even though the bridge is gone.

## Notes

This is an original real-time-strategy campaign inspired by the broad structure of classic 1990s RTS campaigns: faction-specific operations, base-building, commando missions, escorts, timed defence, sabotage, and escalating counterattacks. It does not reuse Red Alert characters, dialogue, mission scripts, or named story events.
