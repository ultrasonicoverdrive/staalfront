## B. Story

- **Situation**: An enemy foundry is producing armoured vehicles faster than the front can destroy them. Sabotage the fuel depot and then neutralise the production yard.
- **Briefing**: `briefings/F1-M09.fountain`
- **Mid-mission twist**: If the fuel depot survives too long, an armoured reserve deploys from underground storage.
- **Story beat**: The player identifies the industrial heart of the enemy offensive.

- **Ending (win)**: The foundry's assembly halls stand cold, the siding is a line of burnt-out tank hulls, and Rusk's count is simple: forty vehicles a week that will never reach the front. In the siding office Mira finds shipping tags on the fuel drums: the broker's mark is not Veyran, and it is the same mark that was on the drums redirected before the first attack.
- **Ending (loss)**: The armoured reserve rolls over the forward base and the foundry keeps its schedule. The front will meet those tanks within the week, and the mission's one lead, the siding office, burns with everything else.

## J. Flow in words

1. **0:00** Briefing. Forward base in the west, river between the player and the foundry. Siege_artillery is new; its range 10 against the enemy's range-7 emplacements is the lesson.
2. **0:00–3:00** Player builds a second refinery and the first siege_artillery (1600 credits). Enemy factories each produce a light_tank every 60 s; Rusk's point is visible in the wave sizes.
3. **3:00** First T_WAVE (12) over the north ford. Towers and the heavy_tank hold.
4. **4:00–6:00** Scout crosses the south ford to the artillery ridge. The moment the depot is sighted (TR_04), the 7:00 reserve timer starts and T_DEPOT_GUARD moves out. The player chooses when discovery happens; scouting with the artillery already on the ridge is the efficient path.
5. **6:00–9:30** Artillery from P_RIDGE_GUN hits the depot from 10 cells; the two depot towers (range 6) cannot reply. Alternatively an engineer with a demolition charge sneaks in along the siding. Depot explodes, tank wagons go with it. Production stops (TR_06, MSG_05).
6. **If the timer expires**: 8 heavy and light tanks emerge from the slag heaps and drive straight at the construction center (87 cells ÷ 1.1 = 79 s after they emerge). They ignore units; the player must kill them in the base with towers, rocket_trooper and heavy_tank while the depot still has to be taken.
7. **9:30–12:00** Waves continue at 180 s but no longer grow. Player builds 2–3 more siege_artillery and heavy_tank, optionally repairs the rail bridge for a direct approach to the gate.
8. **12:00–16:00** Artillery works the west wall emplacements from 10 cells; T_GATE_SORTIE comes out each time guns approach and must be met by the tank screen.
9. **16:00–19:00** Assault through the gate. Factory A, then factory B. Infantry run the siding office for the shipping tags (objective C).
10. **~20:00** Second hall down: win. MSG_07.
11. **End** Shipping tags: the fuel broker's mark is the one from the Missing Manifest interlude. The industrial heart of the offensive is identified, and so is who fuelled it.

## K. After the mission

- **Carry over**: Campaign flag `completed_F1-M09`; flag `m09_reserve_prevented` if `reserve_deployed` = no at win (F1-M10 briefing: the enemy's last heavy armour is still in a sealed tunnel); flag `m09_bridge_repaired` if OBJ_RAIL_BRIDGE was repaired.
- **Evidence**: `evidence_m09` (shipping tags). Adds the broker's mark to the "Archive Key" interlude before F1-M10.
- **Choice**: Continue to F1-M10.
- **Test scenario**:
  1. TR_01 fires once; siege_artillery and advanced_defences are buildable; no superweapon option exists.
  2. `timer_reserve` does not run before any player unit sights OBJ_FUEL_DEPOT; sighting from Z_ARTILLERY_RIDGE at 10 cells starts it and shows 7:00.
  3. Destroying the depot at `timer_reserve` = 1 stops the timer; TR_07 never fires; MSG_05 plays.
  4. Letting the timer expire releases exactly 4 heavy_tank and 4 light_tank at P_TUNNEL_MOUTH, which path to OBJ_CONSTRUCTION_CENTER; destroying the depot afterwards still completes objective A but MSG_05 does not play.
  5. While `depot_down` = no, each living factory spawns 1 light_tank per 60 s; after TR_06 no more are spawned and waves stay at 12.
  6. Destroying both factories before the depot does not win; the win fires only when the depot also dies.
  7. OBJ_RAIL_BRIDGE is impassable at start and passable after an engineer repairs it.
  8. Picking up OBJ_SHIPPING_TAGS sets `evidence_m09`; the depot explosion (radius 3) does not reach the office at (72, 88).
