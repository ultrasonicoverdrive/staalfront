## B. Story

- **Situation**: An enemy foundry is producing armoured vehicles faster than the front can destroy them. Sabotage the fuel depot and then neutralise the production yard.
- **Briefing**: `briefings/F1-M09.fountain`
- **Mid-mission twist**: If the fuel depot survives too long, an armoured reserve deploys from underground storage.
- **Story beat**: The player identifies the industrial heart of the enemy offensive.

## J. Flow in words

1. The briefing establishes the immediate operational problem and states the primary objective clearly.
2. The player starts with a limited force and only partial vision of the map.
3. The first phase teaches the mission's main mechanic: disable production infrastructure before committing to a final assault.
4. Early resistance is deliberately manageable, allowing the player to scout and prepare.
5. The twist occurs around the middle of the target mission length: If the fuel depot survives too long, an armoured reserve deploys from underground storage.
6. The twist opens or reveals a secondary route, new enemy response, or additional condition.
7. The player completes the primary objective while protecting any required mission asset.
8. Victory confirms the local success but adds a new piece to the wider mystery: The player identifies the industrial heart of the enemy offensive.

## Notes

This is an original real-time-strategy campaign inspired by the broad structure of classic 1990s RTS campaigns: faction-specific operations, base-building, commando missions, escorts, timed defence, sabotage, and escalating counterattacks. It does not reuse Red Alert characters, dialogue, mission scripts, or named story events.
