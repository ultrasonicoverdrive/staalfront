## B. Story

- **Situation**: An old stone bridge is the only route for an enemy armoured column. Hold the riverbank while engineers prepare a controlled collapse.
- **Briefing**: `briefings/F2-M04.fountain`
- **Mid-mission twist**: Enemy artillery begins targeting the demolition crew, forcing the player to secure a forward observation hill.
- **Story beat**: The faction commits to a costly defensive strategy.

- **Ending (win)**: The stone bridge falls into the river with the lead of the Compact column on it. The valley road is closed for the season. Sava's report commits the Directorate to a line of bridges, bunkers and observation hills from here to the coast — a defensive strategy that will cost every mission after this one something.
- **Ending (loss)**: Kovac's crew is killed under the shelling, or the column crosses an intact bridge. The west bank is abandoned, the valley road is open, and the Directorate loses the one position that could have stopped armour cheaply.

- `OBJ_OBSERVERS`: two riflemen on the hill at start; interchangeable with any player unit for the hold check.

## J. Flow in words

1. 0:00 (18:00 on the clock) — Briefing. Player sees the base, Kovac at the west abutment, two observers already on the hill across the river, and an enemy probe forming on the east bank. 4000 credits.
2. 0:00–2:30 — Player spends: a fortified_bunker on the west ramp (600, garrison 5 riflemen) and a defence_tower cover the bridge exit; a heavy_gun_team (500) deployed on the ramp handles tanks. The combat_engineers build a second bunker in the field near Kovac.
3. 1:00 — T_WAVE_0 (4 riflemen, 1 tank) crosses. Tutorial-scale fight at the chokepoint.
4. 2:30, 5:00, 7:30 — Waves alternate infantry (8) and armour (9) every 150 s. Everything funnels through three bridge cells; the bunker and gun team earn their cost.
5. 8:00 (10:00 on the clock) — Twist: T_HILL_ASSAULT (6 riflemen, 2 tanks) goes not for the bridge but for the hill. Two observers cannot hold it. The hill falls; howitzers start walking shells onto Kovac's crew every 20 s. Objective B shows as lost.
6. 8:00–10:30 — Player must send a force across the bridge, against the flow of the next wave, to retake the hill: 2 light tanks, rocket troopers and riflemen, or a combat_engineer who throws up a bunker on the hilltop once it is clear. Hill retaken, fire lifts.
7. 9:00 — Sava's halfway line; waves tighten to every 120 s.
8. 10:30–15:30 — Player holds both banks with a split force. Optional: a raid north to the artillery park (6 riflemen, a tower, two howitzers with a 3-cell minimum range — close in fast) removes the artillery threat for good.
9. 16:00 (2:00 on the clock) — Sava warns; T_COLUMN (6 tanks, 6 riflemen, 2 transports) enters and stages on the east bank for 90 s.
10. 17:30 — The column starts across. If the hill is held, the charges go at 18:00 with the column's lead on the bridge.
11. 18:00 — Charges ready. Hill held: bridge falls, win. Hill lost: Kovac waits, 2:00 grace to retake the hill while the column crosses; retake it and the bridge still goes, with whatever is on it.

## K. After the mission

- **Carry over**: Campaign flag `completed_F2-M04`. Flag `artillery_destroyed_F2-M04` (yes/no) carries to F2-M05's briefing (one line about captured gun crews). Evidence flag `evidence_F2-M04` is set when objective D is complete (the park's ammunition shed holds shipping labels from the same broker that appears in F2-M05). No unit carry-over.
- **Choice**: Continue to F2-M05.
- **Test scenario**:
  1. TR_01 fires once; `timer_demolition` shows 18:00; observers visible on the hill; objectives A, B, C shown, D hidden until Z_ARTILLERY_PARK is seen.
  2. Waves: first at 2:30, alternating 8-infantry / 9-armour; from 9:00 the gap shrinks to 120 s; no new waves after the 2:00 mark.
  3. At 10:00 remaining, T_HILL_ASSAULT spawns and goes to the hill, not the bridge.
  4. Pull the observers off the hill before the assault arrives: `hill_held` flips to no as soon as an enemy unit stands on an empty hill; MSG_04; salvoes begin 20 s later, the first salvo landing within radius 4 of Kovac.
  5. Put one player unit back on an enemy-free hill: `hill_held` = yes, MSG_05, salvoes stop. Repeat the loss: salvoes resume without MSG_04.
  6. Kill Kovac with a shell or a tank: MSG_09, lose. Kovac has 200 HP and survives a near miss.
  7. Destroy both howitzers: objective D complete, salvoes stop permanently, later hill losses produce no artillery.
  8. Let the clock reach 0:00 with the hill held: all three bridge segments vanish, anything in Z_BRIDGE dies, MSG_08, win.
  9. Let the clock reach 0:00 with the hill lost: MSG_07, a 2:00 grace clock; retake the hill at 0:30 of grace: bridge falls, win. Let grace expire: MSG_10, lose.
  10. Let a single enemy rifleman reach Z_START at any time: MSG_10, lose.
  11. Bridge segments cannot be damaged by player or enemy fire during the mission.

## Notes

This is an original real-time-strategy campaign inspired by the broad structure of classic 1990s RTS campaigns: faction-specific operations, base-building, commando missions, escorts, timed defence, sabotage, and escalating counterattacks. It does not reuse Red Alert characters, dialogue, mission scripts, or named story events.
