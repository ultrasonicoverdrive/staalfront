## B. Story

- **Situation**: A border garrison is cut off after an unidentified force attacks a relay station. Secure the area and establish a foothold before the enemy can regroup.
- **Briefing**: `briefings/F1-M01.fountain`
- **Mid-mission twist**: The enemy has only a small outpost; at 5:00 a second patrol arrives from the eastern road.
- **Story beat**: The player discovers the war is coordinated, not a random border clash.

| MSG_07 | MIRA | "Junction clear. I pulled their patrol schedule off the camp radio. Both groups were ordered here two days ago, with our arrival time on it." |
| MSG_08 | OPERATIONS | "Garrison overrun. Field command orders withdrawal to the southern ridge." |

- **Ending (win)**: The junction holds and the relay road is open again. The patrol schedule recovered from the enemy camp was written two days before the relay went dark and names the hour of the Compact's arrival. This was not a border clash that got out of hand; someone timed it.
- **Ending (loss)**: The base is overrun before the junction is taken. Field command pulls the survivors back to the southern ridge, the relay road stays closed, and the units north of the ridge keep operating on stale orders.

## C. Map

- **Map**: new
- **Size and terrain**: Small, 64×64 cells; broken farmland, a road junction at the centre, low ridge to the north.
- **Sketch**: create `missions/F1-M01-map.png` during map production.
- **Sketch description**: Player starts in the south-west corner on flat farmland with a cleared build area. An ore field sits on a low ridge directly north of the start (north-west quarter). A dirt road runs from the start north-east to a crossroads at map centre, where the enemy camp sits in a ring of low farm walls. From the crossroads one road continues east to the map edge (enemy patrol entry) and one north to the ridge pass. Scattered hedgerows and two ruined barns south of the crossroads give infantry cover. The north-east quarter is empty hills.

## J. Flow in words

1. 0:00 — Briefing, MSG_01/02. Player sees construction_center, 5 riflemen and the ore ridge to the north. Objective A shown.
2. 0:30–3:00 — Player builds power_plant and barracks (1 power_plant covers both). If nothing is placed by 3:00, MSG_03 nudges the order.
3. 3:00–4:30 — TR_03: base built, MSG_04 reveals the camp at the junction, objective B shown. Player trains riflemen (target 8–10) and may build a refinery on the ridge (harvester comes with it).
4. 4:30 — Player moves infantry towards Z_FARM_WALLS; T_CAMP_GUARD reacts only inside Z_JUNCTION.
5. 5:00 — TR_05: `timer_patrol` expires, MSG_05/06, T_EAST_PATROL (5 units, D1 size) enters from P_EAST_ENTRY and heads for the junction. Z_EAST_ROAD revealed.
6. 5:30–8:00 — Player either holds at the farm walls and lets the patrol come, or takes the camp first and defends the junction. The light tank in each group is the threat; massed rifles handle it at D1.
7. 8:00–10:00 — Player clears the remaining camp guards and the patrol remnants. Harvester keeps running; a second barracks batch replaces losses.
8. 10:00–12:00 — TR_06: junction clear with both groups destroyed. MSG_07: Mira reads the patrol schedule from `OBJ_CAMP_RADIO`. Win.
9. Loss branch: if the patrol overruns the base and the construction_center falls without a barracks (TR_07), MSG_08 and withdrawal.

## K. After the mission

- **Carry over**: Campaign flag `completed_F1-M01` and `evidence_m01_schedule` (always set on win; it unlocks a line in the F1-M02 briefing about pre-dated orders). Surviving units do not carry over.
- **Choice**: Continue to F1-M02.
- **Test scenario**:
  1. TR_01 fires once and both `timer_patrol` and `timer_tutorial` start at 5:00 and 3:00.
  2. Build nothing for 3:00: MSG_03 plays exactly once; build a power_plant at 2:50: MSG_03 never plays.
  3. Build power_plant and barracks: TR_03 fires, objective A completes, Z_JUNCTION revealed, objective B appears.
  4. At 5:00 T_EAST_PATROL spawns at P_EAST_ENTRY with 4 rifleman + 1 light_tank and reaches P_JUNCTION within 60 s; MSG_05 and MSG_06 play once.
  5. Clear Z_JUNCTION before 5:00: no win until the patrol has arrived and is destroyed.
  6. Destroy T_CAMP_GUARD and T_EAST_PATROL after base built: TR_06 fires, MSG_07 plays, `evidence_m01_schedule` set, win screen.
  7. Lose the construction_center with no barracks and 300 credits: TR_07 fires, MSG_08, loss. With a barracks alive: no loss.
  8. Lose all riflemen with no barracks: TR_08 fires. With a barracks alive: no loss.

## Notes

This is an original real-time-strategy campaign inspired by the broad structure of classic 1990s RTS campaigns: faction-specific operations, base-building, commando missions, escorts, timed defence, sabotage, and escalating counterattacks. It does not reuse Red Alert characters, dialogue, mission scripts, or named story events.

**Timing check** (speeds from `units.md`; distance in cells ÷ speed = time; retuned 17-10-2026, Tijmen approved):
- P_EAST_ENTRY (63, 32) to P_JUNCTION (32, 32): 31 cells ÷ 0.7 (rifleman, the slowest) = 44 s, so the "within 60 s" of test 4 holds. The light tank alone needs 16 s.
- Player start (8, 56) to P_JUNCTION (32, 32): 34 cells ÷ 0.7 = 49 s.
- `timer_patrol` (5:00) and `timer_tutorial` (3:00) are pacing timers and the 12 min target is build-and-fight time. No number changed.
