## B. Story

- **Situation**: A coastal pumping station is transferring fuel to a fleet of unmarked vessels. Capture the control building before the transfer completes.
- **Briefing**: `briefings/F2-M06.fountain`
- **Mid-mission twist**: The station's operators scuttle the docks, forcing an inland approach to the control room.
- **Story beat**: The conflict expands to the coast and threatens neutral shipping.

- **Ending (win)**: The pumps are stopped with the vessels still moored. Their hulls carry no markings and their manifests name a logistics broker the Directorate has never fought. The war has reached the coast, and neutral shipping lanes now run through it.
- **Ending (loss)**: The unmarked fleet slips out past the breakwater with full tanks. Sava orders the survivors inland. The fuel will feed someone's offensive, and the Directorate still does not know whose.

## C. Map

- **Map**: new
- **Size and terrain**: Large, 112×112 cells. Coastline runs along the whole east edge and the south-east corner. Pumping station compound in the north-east on a raised concrete apron; a dock platform east of it in the water, joined to the mainland by a three-segment causeway. Inland service works (pipe yards, valve houses, rail spur) fill the centre and north-west. Storage tank farm between service works and station. Player start on dry ground in the south-west with a sheltered inlet to its south-east suitable for a naval_yard.
- **Sketch**: create `missions/F2-M06-map.png` during map production.
- **Sketch description**: Draw the map as a bay opening to the east. Bottom-left: flat ground with an ore field and a small inlet. A paved coastal road leads north-east along the shore to the docks (bottom-right to right edge). A second, dirt road leads north through the service works (centre) to the tank farm and the station gate (top-right). The station sits on a concrete apron with the control building at its centre, four shore-facing gun positions, and the causeway leading off its east side to a dock platform where two unmarked vessels are moored. The inlet near the player start connects by a narrow water channel along the south and east edges to the water below the station apron.

## J. Flow in words

1. 0:00 — Briefing. Base visible with the coast road running north-east. Timer shows 18:00. MSG_04 offers the naval yard.
2. 0:00–3:00 — Player builds barracks/vehicle_factory, probably a naval_yard (1000) in Z_INLET, and scouts the coast road.
3. 3:00–6:00 — The force reaches the dock landing; T_DOCK_GUARD and two towers are the first fight. The moment a unit steps onto the docks or causeway, the three bridge segments blow (TR_05). Anyone on them dies. Petrov calls the inland route.
4. 6:00 — Enemy AI switches to attacks. Harassment teams come down the dirt road every two minutes. Player must turn around, cross back west, and enter the service works at P_WORKS_GATE_SOUTH.
5. 7:00–11:00 — Service works: towers, decoys and T_VALVE_HOLD at the valve house. Heavy_gun_teams and rocket troopers do the work; decoys waste shots if the player fires blindly. MSG_05 at 9:00 remaining.
6. 10:00 — T_COUNTER_1 (13 units) arrives from the north road into the valve house area. If the player has stalled here, this is the pressure point.
7. 11:00–14:00 — Tank farm. Destroying fuel depots blows up adjacent shore towers (TR_15), opening Z_STATION_WATER for gunboats. Gunboats at P_GUNBOAT_STAND shell the station garrison and the enemy base; T_SHORE_RESPONSE answers.
8. 14:00–16:00 — Station gate assault with gunboat support. MSG_06 at 3:00 remaining. Engineer walks to P_CONTROL and captures (TR_09); the timer stops.
9. 16:00–17:30 — T_RECAPTURE (16 units, with an engineer) comes from the enemy barracks and factory next door. Player holds the building for 90 s with garrisoned bunkers and tanks.
10. ~18:00 — MSG_08, win. Optional: a gunboat sinks OBJ_VESSEL_1 for the registry (evidence_F2-M06).

## K. After the mission

- **Carry over**: Campaign flag `completed_F2-M06`. Evidence flag `evidence_F2-M06` if a vessel was sunk (TR_08); it changes Lenka's opening line in the F2-M07 briefing to mention the shell company. Surviving units do not carry over.
- **Choice**: Continue to F2-M07.
- **Test scenario**:
  1. TR_01 fires once; `timer_transfer` shows 18:00 and counts down.
  2. Walk one rifleman onto Z_DOCKS: all three OBJ_CAUSEWAY segments are destroyed, the rifleman on the segment dies, MSG_03 plays, objective B appears; a second entry does nothing.
  3. Do not go near the docks: at 8:00 elapsed (timer 10:00) TR_06 scuttles the causeway anyway.
  4. Repair the causeway with an engineer after the twist: it is possible but takes 3 × 60 s and the dock route still ends at the station's east towers.
  5. Let the timer expire with the building uncaptured: MSG_09 and loss; capture at 0:05 remaining: timer stops, TR_09 fires.
  6. Capture, then let T_RECAPTURE's engineer retake the building: TR_12 restarts the timer at 2:00; recapture again and hold 90 s: win.
  7. Destroy OBJ_CONTROL_BUILDING with gunboat fire: immediate loss (TR_11).
  8. Place a naval_yard outside Z_INLET: placement refused.
  9. Sink OBJ_VESSEL_2 with a gunboat: MSG_10 once, `evidence_F2-M06` set; sinking the other vessel afterwards does nothing.
  10. Destroy OBJ_TANK_3: towers within 3 cells take 300 damage.

## Notes

This is an original real-time-strategy campaign inspired by the broad structure of classic 1990s RTS campaigns: faction-specific operations, base-building, commando missions, escorts, timed defence, sabotage, and escalating counterattacks. It does not reuse Red Alert characters, dialogue, mission scripts, or named story events.
