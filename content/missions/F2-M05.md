## B. Story

- **Situation**: Reports say a major arsenal is ready to supply the enemy. Infiltrate it and determine whether the weapons are real or staged.
- **Briefing**: `briefings/F2-M05.fountain`
- **Mid-mission twist**: The arsenal is mostly empty; the real objective is a ledger showing who paid for the shipment.
- **Story beat**: The war's supply chain points beyond either faction.

- **Ending (win)**: The team walks out of the dry canal with the ledger. The arsenal that justified a Compact offensive never held a shell; the invoices were paid by a logistics broker with no flag. Lenka now has a thread leading out of the war entirely, and Sava, for the first time, orders her to follow it rather than file it.
- **Ending (loss)**: The archive burns, or the team is lost in the compound. Compact command announces that a Veyran raid destroyed a "fully stocked arsenal"; the empty sheds are never seen, and the story of who paid for them goes up with the building.

## J. Flow in words

1. 0:00 — Briefing. Night. Eleven units in a ditch south of a walled compound; two of them are demolition teams that can each remove one structure.
2. 0:30–2:30 — Scout goes forward and sees the south gate: two gun emplacements, guardhouse, a tank asleep in the motor pool. The scout also finds (or the player stumbles on) the dry canal to the west with a culvert into the compound wall.
3. 2:30–4:30 — Choice. Frontal: two demolition teams on the two gate guns, then a firefight with the gate pair and the woken tank (alarm climbs fast). Quiet: up the canal, through the culvert into the west yard, nobody shooting yet. A hybrid: one demolition team on the generator from the canal side kills the guns and the lights (objective B) and keeps the second charge for the way out.
4. ~4:00 — First player unit steps into the shed yard. Twist: the sheds are empty. Lenka points to the archive at the back. Objectives C and D appear, E has been there since the start.
5. 4:00–6:00 — Archive yard: two riflemen on the door, a lamp (if the generator still runs). The player must not use the demolition teams or rockets near the archive. Rifles only; the engineer can patch the building if a stray rocket hits it.
6. ~6:00 — Rifleman walks onto the ledger. Full alarm. 4:00 exfil clock. Thirty seconds later a response team enters the north gate; reserve squads come out of the guardhouse every 60 s (two at most).
7. 6:00–8:30 — Run for the culvert (archive yard to culvert 24 cells, canal to the mouth about 35 cells: 59 cells ÷ 0.7 = 1:24 of walking, so the 4:00 clock leaves about 2:30 for fighting and detours). The canal banks block sight; once inside the canal the team is out of the garrison's view. The carrier dies → the book drops where he fell, anyone can pick it up again.
8. 10:00 — If the clock expires, four riflemen come down the canal from the culvert behind the team; the extraction is still reachable, just no longer quiet.
9. ~8:00–9:30 — Carrier enters the canal mouth. Sava closes: an empty arsenal, a full ledger, a broker's name.
