## B. Story

- **Situation**: A convoy carrying captured records must reach headquarters through a mountain pass before the weather closes it.
- **Briefing**: `briefings/F2-M08.fountain`
- **Mid-mission twist**: The enemy attacks the bridge behind the convoy, dividing the escort from its rear guard.
- **Story beat**: The player can now name the network funding both sides' escalation.

- **Ending (win)**: Two or three trucks grind out of the pass as the storm closes it behind them. Copied ledgers, cross-checked against the originals held at the staging area, tie shell companies on both sides of the border to the same logistics broker. For the first time the Directorate can name who is funding the escalation.
- **Ending (loss)**: The records are lost in the snow, or the staging headquarters that held the originals is overrun. The copies that survive cannot be verified, and the broker remains a rumour.

|---|---|---|---|

| D | Recover the courier's satchel at the saddle. | no | TR_18 |

**Win**: at least two of OBJ_TRUCK_1…3 have entered Z_PASS_EXIT, OBJ_STAGING_HQ is alive, and either every truck is accounted for (arrived or lost) or `timer_weather` has expired.
**Lose**: two trucks are destroyed; or `timer_weather` expires with fewer than two trucks arrived; or OBJ_STAGING_HQ is destroyed.

## J. Flow in words

1. 0:00 — Briefing. Convoy and escort at the gate, rear guard in the gate zone, timer 22:00. The player has a working base but no time to grow it much; credits go into bunkers, a repair_truck or two, and towers at the gate.
2. 0:00–3:00 — First staging wave (13 units) is released on the north-west track at 3:00 (TR_02) and reaches the gate about 1:10 later (50 cells ÷ 0.7). The player decides how much of the escort stays. Convoy starts east along the gorge road.
3. 3:00–8:00 — Road west and the bridge: quiet. Repair_truck keeps the light tanks topped up. Second wave at 6:00 from the south-west.
4. 10:00 — T_BRIDGE_RAIDERS come up the ravine behind the convoy, 12 rocket troopers and 2 tanks. The bridge is their target (TR_03); the convoy that has already crossed is fine, anything still west is cut off. Petrov calls it (MSG_03), Sava sets the dilemma (MSG_04). Waves 3 and 4 hit the staging area at 9:00 and 12:00 with the escort gone.
5. 11:00 — Storm warning at 11:00 remaining (MSG_05). Player chooses: engineers rebuild the bridge (2 × 60 s under fire) to bring the rear guard forward, or the rear guard holds the plateau alone.
6. 12:00–15:00 — Road mid: T_ROAD_PICKET, then the ravine mouth where surviving raiders chase the convoy. Repair_truck matters here.
7. 15:00–17:00 — Hairpin: six rocket troopers in the rocks fire down on the trucks (TR_05). A truck can die here if the escort is not ahead of it. MSG_07 on the first loss.
8. 16:00 — T_PASS_BLOCK (14 units) comes in from the pass exit and pushes west to the saddle (TR_07); the convoy must break through it, not outlast it.
9. 18:00–20:00 — Saddle fight; visibility drops (MSG_06). Trucks roll into the exit zone one at a time (TR_11). Optional satchel from the courier transport.
10. ~21:00 — Second (or third) truck exits: MSG_08, win. Waves 5 and 6 at 15:00 and 18:00 test whether the rear guard still holds the HQ.

## K. After the mission

- **Carry over**: Campaign flag `completed_F2-M08`; flag `ledger_trucks_arrived` = 2 or 3 (3 adds a line to the "Ledger Copies" interlude). Evidence flag `evidence_F2-M08` if the courier's satchel was recovered; it names the broker one mission early in the F2-M09 briefing. Surviving units do not carry over.
- **Choice**: Continue to F2-M09.
- **Test scenario**:
  1. TR_01 fires once; `timer_weather` shows 22:00; the first staging wave is released at 3:00 on the north-west track, the second at 6:00 on the south-west track.
  2. At 10:00 T_BRIDGE_RAIDERS come up the ravine and destroy both segments; MSG_03 then MSG_04 once; objective C appears.
  3. Destroy both bridge segments yourself before 10:00: TR_03 does not fire (raiders never spawn); TR_04 fires.
  4. Repair both segments: objective C disappears; raiders cannot blow it a second time (team is spent).
  5. Lose two trucks: immediate loss (TR_10). Lose one: MSG_07 once, mission continues; two arrivals then win.
  6. All three trucks arrive at 15:00: win immediately (TR_12), T_PASS_BLOCK at 16:00 never spawns.
  7. Two trucks arrive, third still on the road when the timer expires: TR_13 win. One truck arrived at expiry: TR_14 loss.
  8. Let the staging HQ fall while the convoy is at the saddle: TR_15 loss, MSG_10.
  9. Count staging waves: exactly 6, alternating entries, wave 4–6 include one heavy_tank.
  10. Destroy the parked transport at the saddle and pick up the satchel: MSG_12 once, `evidence_F2-M08` set.

## Notes

This is an original real-time-strategy campaign inspired by the broad structure of classic 1990s RTS campaigns: faction-specific operations, base-building, commando missions, escorts, timed defence, sabotage, and escalating counterattacks. It does not reuse Red Alert characters, dialogue, mission scripts, or named story events.
