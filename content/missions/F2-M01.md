## B. Story

- **Situation**: A defensive column finds an abandoned junction that could anchor a new front. Secure the junction and drive off the scattered raiders.
- **Briefing**: `briefings/F2-M01.fountain`
- **Mid-mission twist**: A raider group emerges from a snowbound service tunnel after the first objective is nearly complete.
- **Story beat**: The second faction enters the war believing it is responding to an attack.

- **Ending (win)**: The junction is held and the raiders are driven back into the tunnel. Among their abandoned gear Dravec finds Compact-pattern radios and Veyran-sealed movement orders that nobody at command issued. Sava reports a Meridian provocation; the Directorate mobilises in response to an attack that, on paper, both sides appear to have ordered.
- **Ending (loss)**: The construction center burns and the column retreats to the mountain pass. The junction stays in raider hands, the north road stays open to the Compact, and the Directorate's first move of the war is a withdrawal.

|---|---|---|---|

**Win**: `outpost_cleared` = yes, T_RAIDERS eliminated, and no enemy unit inside Z_JUNCTION or Z_OUTPOST (TR_09).
**Lose**: OBJ_YARD is destroyed and the player has no rifleman alive and no barracks (TR_10, or the TR_11 follow-up check).

## J. Flow in words

1. 0:00 — Briefing. Player sees the yard, the refinery, the harvester at work and 6 riflemen. Objective A asks for a power plant and a barracks.
2. 0:30–2:30 — Player places power_plant and barracks near the yard (tutorial hints on placement and the build radius of 8 cells). Harvester keeps income flowing.
3. 2:30 — Objective A completes; Sava points at the outpost. Z_OUTPOST is revealed: four structures, six riflemen, one light tank. Player begins training riflemen (6–8 more).
4. 4:00 — `timer_relief` expires; three riflemen and a light tank arrive from the east road and attack-move toward the yard. The player's riflemen meet them around the junction. First real fight.
5. 5:00–8:00 — Player pushes north with 10–14 riflemen, kills the guard force and starts on the outpost structures. Fuel depots explode and damage anything within 3 cells, a lesson in standing back.
6. ~8:30 — Three of four structures are down. Dravec calls the tunnel. Twenty seconds later T_RAIDERS (6 riflemen, 1 light tank) spawn north-west of the yard and go for the harvester and refinery. Objective C appears.
7. 8:30–10:30 — Player splits: a few riflemen finish the last outpost structure, the rest run back (or fresh riflemen from the barracks hold) to defend the base. Raiders retreat to the tunnel when reduced to 2 units.
8. 10:30–11:30 — Last outpost structure falls (objective B). Player hunts the remaining raiders; T_RAIDERS reaches 0 alive.
9. ~12:00 — No enemy in the junction or outpost; Sava closes the mission with the first hint that the ambush was staged.

  2. Build power_plant only: objective A stays open. Add barracks: TR_02 fires, MSG_03 plays, Z_OUTPOST revealed.
  3. Build barracks before power_plant: TR_02 still fires only when both exist.
  4. At 4:00 T_EAST_RELIEF (3 riflemen, 1 light_tank) enters at P_EAST_ENTRY and attack-moves toward the yard.
  5. Destroy 3 of 4 outpost structures in any order: TR_05 fires exactly once, MSG_04 plays, Z_TUNNEL_MOUTH revealed, and T_RAIDERS spawns at P_TUNNEL 20 s later.
  6. Destroy the last two structures within the same second: `outpost_remaining` reaches 0, TR_07 fires, and TR_05/TR_06 still run once (raiders still spawn).
  7. Kill all T_RAIDERS before the last structure falls: win does not fire until `outpost_cleared` = yes.
  8. Win fires only when outpost cleared, raiders dead and Z_JUNCTION/Z_OUTPOST empty of enemies.
  9. Destroy OBJ_YARD with no riflemen and no barracks: MSG_08 and lose. Destroy it with a barracks standing: no immediate loss; 120 s later with no riflemen and no barracks, lose.
  10. Losing the harvester plays MSG_07 once and does not end the mission.
