## B. Story

- **Situation**: A supply convoy carrying fuel and field radios must cross a long valley to reach the forward garrison.
- **Briefing**: `briefings/F1-M03.fountain`
- **Mid-mission twist**: At the halfway point, the main road is blocked; the convoy must take a narrow forest route.
- **Story beat**: The faction's logistics network becomes the campaign's lifeline.

- **Ending (win)**: At least two trucks reach the northern garrison. Rusk's manifest shows the fuel and radios he loaded were already short: a second shipment on the same manifest was redirected a week before the relay attack. The Compact's supply lines are now the thing the enemy is actually fighting, and the thing the campaign will have to defend.
- **Ending (loss)**: Fewer than two trucks survive. Rusk turns the remainder back, the northern garrison goes dark two days later, and the valley road belongs to whoever blocked it.

## C. Map

- **Map**: new
- **Size and terrain**: Large, 128×64 cells; valley road from west to east, forest bypass in the north, garrison at the eastern edge.
- **Sketch**: create `missions/F1-M03-map.png` during map production.
- **Sketch description**: A single valley road runs west to east along the map's lower third, between a ridge to the south (enemy observation and ambush ground) and a river to the north. At the map's horizontal midpoint the road passes through a rock cut; this is where the landslide falls. North of the cut, a dense forest fills the upper half of the map between x=56 and x=104, crossed by one forestry track that leaves the road at x=52, loops north through the trees and rejoins the road at x=104. The player's staging post is at the west edge on the road. The garrison is a walled compound at the east edge. Two side valleys open from the southern ridge onto the road at x=36 and x=84: enemy entry points. A clearing with a woodcutter's hut sits in the middle of the forest track.

## J. Flow in words

1. 0:00 — Briefing, MSG_01–03. Convoy of 4 trucks starts rolling east at once; player has 2 tanks, 6 rifles, 2 rocket troopers and the repair truck. MSG_04 at 0:20 explains the repair truck.
2. 1:00–3:00 — T_RIDGE_WATCH on the ridge above P_WP1 fires at anything that comes close but does not chase. Player learns the convoy halts (TR_04) when enemies are within 7 cells and must clear the ridge or screen it.
3. 3:00–5:00 — TR_03 at P_WP1: T_AMBUSH_A (5 units) comes out of the southern side valley and goes for the trucks. Rocket troopers handle the tank; repair truck patches the escort tanks after.
4. 5:00–7:30 — Quiet stretch to P_WP2. Player can spend credits on a second repair_truck or more rocket troopers at the staging post and send them forward with the light_transport if built.
5. 7:30–8:00 — TR_05 at P_HALFWAY (the halfway point in distance and time): landslide blocks the cut, MSG_06/07, forest track revealed, convoy turns back to P_TRACK_START.
6. 8:00–10:30 — Convoy enters the forest (TR_06). T_AMBUSH_B (6 units) follows from behind; `timer_rush` starts. Player must guard the rear while scouting ahead to Z_CLEARING.
7. 10:30–12:30 — Z_CLEARING ambush (T_CLEARING, 6 units, rockets in the trees). Player clears it before the trucks arrive, or the trucks halt in the one-lane track with T_AMBUSH_B behind them.
8. 12:30–14:30 — TR_08: T_FINAL_RUSH (6 units) waits at P_TRACK_END. Player pushes through with the tanks and clears the exit so the trucks can leave the forest.
9. 14:30–16:00 — Trucks enter Z_GARRISON (TR_09 counts each). TR_10 win with ≥2 delivered; MSG_09.
10. Loss branch: third truck lost (TR_11) at any point; MSG_10.

## K. After the mission

- **Carry over**: Campaign flag `completed_F1-M03`, `evidence_m03_manifest` (set on win; feeds the interlude "The Missing Manifest"), and `trucks_delivered` (2–4) as a number for the F1-M04 briefing line. No unit carry-over.
- **Choice**: Continue to F1-M04.
- **Test scenario**:
  1. TR_01 fires once; 4 supply_truck start moving east within 5 s without player input.
  2. Place a light_tank 6 cells from the convoy: `convoy_halted` stays no. Place an Enemy 1 rifleman 6 cells away: trucks stop within 1 s; remove it: trucks resume after 5 s.
  3. Lead truck reaches P_WP1: T_AMBUSH_A spawns at P_ENTRY_A with 4 rifleman + 1 light_tank and targets a supply_truck before any escort unit.
  4. Lead truck reaches P_HALFWAY: Z_ROCK_CUT becomes impassable for all units, MSG_06 and MSG_07 play once, T_CONVOY reroutes via P_TRACK_START without manual orders.
  5. Convoy enters Z_FOREST_BYPASS: T_AMBUSH_B spawns and `timer_rush` runs 2:30; T_FINAL_RUSH appears at P_TRACK_END when it expires.
  6. Destroy 2 trucks: `trucks_alive` = 2, no loss, MSG_08 played once. Destroy a third: TR_11 loss immediately, even if 1 truck is already in Z_GARRISON.
  7. Deliver 2 trucks with a third still alive on the track: no win until it enters Z_GARRISON or dies (if it dies with 2 delivered, `trucks_alive` = 2 and TR_10 then fires).
  8. Build a repair_truck at the vehicle_factory: allowed; attempt to build a defence_tower or heavy_tank: not in the build menu.

## Notes

This is an original real-time-strategy campaign inspired by the broad structure of classic 1990s RTS campaigns: faction-specific operations, base-building, commando missions, escorts, timed defence, sabotage, and escalating counterattacks. It does not reuse Red Alert characters, dialogue, mission scripts, or named story events.
