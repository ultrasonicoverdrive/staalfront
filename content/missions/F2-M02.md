## B. Story

- **Situation**: Enemy uniforms and forged orders have appeared in several villages. Inspect three signal posts and identify the one transmitting authentic hostile commands.
- **Briefing**: `briefings/F2-M02.fountain`
- **Mid-mission twist**: One apparently hostile post is operated by frightened local volunteers; destroying it causes a mission penalty.
- **Story beat**: The player learns that both sides are being pushed toward escalation.

- **Ending (win)**: The mill transmitter is destroyed with its origin codes in Lenka's hands. The codes match orders sent to Meridian villages as well as Veyran ones. Sava files the report he was told to file, and a second, private note: both sides are receiving the same forged commands from the same hand.
- **Ending (loss)**: The team is withdrawn before the source is confirmed. A fresh batch of forged orders reaches the valley the next morning, and two villages that had stayed quiet take up arms. The Directorate's intelligence picture is now worse than before the mission.

**Inspection rule (mission-specific)**: in this mission an engineer entering any of the three posts does **not** capture it. Entering sets the post's `inspected_*` variable and the engineer steps back out one cell. Posts A and C remain Enemy 1 and may be attacked afterwards; post B remains Neutral.

## J. Flow in words

1. 0:00 — Briefing. Three unidentified markers on the minimap. Player has 2 engineers, 2 scouts, 8 riflemen and no base.
2. 0:30–3:00 — Scouts go ahead through the hedgerows. Enemy scouts patrol the lanes; staying in hedge cells keeps the player's scouts hidden. First sighting raises the alarm by one.
3. 3:00–5:30 — Hollin. Four riflemen on the square. Player kills or draws them off, an engineer enters OBJ_POST_A. Lenka: a relay, not a source. Markers update.
4. 5:30–8:00 — The east farm. The post looks exactly like Hollin's. Civilians run when the riflemen approach. Engineer enters OBJ_POST_B. Twist: frightened volunteers. Player who shoots first fails objective C and gets Sava's reprimand.
5. 8:00–10:00 — The mill. Five riflemen and a light tank hold the stone bridge. Player uses the stream bank hedgerows to bring riflemen across; the tank is the real threat with no anti-armour yet, so numbers and the engineers' timing matter.
6. ~10:00 — Engineer enters OBJ_POST_C. Lenka confirms the source and warns of the response team. Objective B appears. Objective A completes if the other two posts are done.
7. 10:45 — T_RESPONSE (6 riflemen, 1 light tank) enters from the north road and heads for the mill.
8. 10:45–13:30 — Player destroys OBJ_POST_C with riflemen (400 HP, roughly 40 s of fire from 6 riflemen) while holding the mill yard against the response team.
9. ~14:30 — Response team broken, transmitter silent. Sava closes: someone is writing orders for both armies.

  2. Engineer enters OBJ_POST_A: post stays Enemy 1, `inspected_A` = yes, MSG_03 plays, T_GUARD_A withdraws to P_PATROL_WEST.
  3. Engineer enters OBJ_POST_B: post stays Neutral, MSG_04 plays, civilians stop fleeing.
  4. Destroy OBJ_POST_B (before or after inspection): MSG_06 plays, objective C fails, `civilian_harm_F2-M02` = yes, mission continues and can still be won.
  5. Kill 2 volunteers with rifle fire without touching the post: same penalty as 4; killing 1 does not trigger it.
  6. Destroy OBJ_POST_C before any engineer enters it: MSG_07 and lose. Same for OBJ_POST_A.
  7. Engineer enters OBJ_POST_C: MSG_05, objective B appears, T_RESPONSE spawns at P_NORTH_ENTRY 45 s later and moves to the mill.
  8. Destroy OBJ_POST_C after inspection but before inspecting A or B: objective B completes, win waits until objective A completes and T_RESPONSE is dead.
  9. Both engineers killed with objective A open: lose. One engineer killed: no effect.
  10. Let an enemy scout see a player unit twice (≥ 30 s apart): T_HUNTERS (2 riflemen) leaves the mill; fires only once.
