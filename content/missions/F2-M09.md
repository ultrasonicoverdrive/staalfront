## B. Story

- **Situation**: An enemy command complex coordinates unmanned artillery and armoured columns. Disable its power relays and command uplink before attacking the central compound.
- **Briefing**: `briefings/F2-M09.fountain`
- **Mid-mission twist**: The complex switches to a local generator and begins a final artillery barrage if the uplink is not destroyed quickly.
- **Story beat**: The faction prepares a strike on the architects of the war.

- **Ending (win)**: With the relays, uplink and compound silenced, the Directorate reads the complex's files: the unmanned batteries were built and maintained under contract with the broker named in the ledgers, and his coordination traffic points to a single hub in the Black Basin. Sava orders the strike planned.
- **Ending (loss)**: The uplink stayed alive long enough for the backup generator to bring the batteries on line, and the Directorate's forward base was shelled flat. The complex keeps directing fire across the front, and the architects of the war remain out of reach.

## C. Map

Player base in the south-west corner on open ground with an ore field. **Key objects**:
- `OBJ_GENERATOR_1`, `OBJ_GENERATOR_2`, `OBJ_GENERATOR_3`: generator, HP 400, one per relay yard. While any is alive, OBJ_UPLINK takes no damage. Each one destroyed removes power from its yard's two defence_towers (they stop firing).
- `OBJ_UPLINK`: relay_transmitter on the ridge, HP 400. Invulnerable until all three generators are dead. While alive, OBJ_COMMAND_BUILDING is invulnerable and the batteries can fire once powered.
- `OBJ_LOCAL_GENERATOR`: generator inside the compound, starts off. Switched on by TR_08; once on, powers OBJ_BATTERY_1…4. Destroying it also stops the barrage.
- `OBJ_BATTERY_1` … `OBJ_BATTERY_4`: unmanned_artillery, HP 300, in the battery ring. Dormant; after TR_08 each fires every 20 s at P_BARRAGE_AIM.
- `OBJ_COMMAND_BUILDING`: enemy construction_center at P_COMMAND. Invulnerable while OBJ_UPLINK is alive. Destroy or capture to finish.
- `OBJ_BATTERY_PLANS`: evidence_crate in the compound. Optional; foreshadows F2-M10.

## I. Objectives (as the player sees them)

| ID | Text (English) | Required? | Completed by trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Destroy the three power relays (generators) in the west, north and east sub-stations. The uplink cannot be damaged while any relay runs. | yes | TR_05 |
| B | Destroy the uplink on the slag ridge. After the last relay falls you have 5:00 before the backup generator powers the batteries. | yes | TR_07 |
| C | Destroy or capture the command building in the central compound. Its doors stay sealed while the uplink is alive. | yes | TR_11 |
| D | The batteries are shelling your base. Destroy the uplink or the local generator inside the compound to stop it. | conditional | TR_10 (or TR_07) |
| E | Recover the battery blueprints from the compound. | no | TR_14 |

**Win**: OBJ_GENERATOR_1…3 destroyed, OBJ_UPLINK destroyed, and OBJ_COMMAND_BUILDING destroyed or owned by the Player.
**Lose**: the Player has no construction_center, vehicle_factory or barracks; or the Player has no construction_center, no combat units and fewer than 1500 credits.

## J. Flow in words

1. 0:00 — Briefing. Base, one siege_artillery, three objectives listed in order. The player builds a radar and a second siege gun; the first wave (13) comes through the east underpass at 4:00.
2. 2:00–6:00 — Scout or radar finds Z_RELAY_W just beyond the western underpass. The artillery pits are seen on the way; Lenka's foreshadowing line (MSG_03).
3. 6:00–9:00 — Relay W: siege_artillery from P_ARTY_POSITION_W outranges the two towers; infantry mops up the guard. T_RELAY_RELIEF arrives from the compound as the generator takes damage. MSG_04.
4. 8:00, 12:00, 16:00, 20:00 — further waves alternate underpasses; advanced_defences at the base make them survivable.
5. 9:00–13:00 — Relay N along the top of the map, then Relay E across the long east side (or in the reverse order). Heavy tanks (new) carry the push; repair_truck follows.
6. ~13:00 — Third generator dies: MSG_05, countdown 5:00. T_UPLINK_DEFENCE moves to the ridge foot; the slope is covered by two advanced_defences (range 7) and snipers.
7. 13:00–17:00 — The race: siege guns from (86, 34) reach the advanced_defences and the uplink (range 10); tanks hold the ridge foot. Players who are slow see MSG_06 at 2:30 and, at 0:00, the barrage (MSG_07): four shells into the base every 20 s, 150 damage each.
8. ~17:00 — Uplink falls (MSG_08): barrage stops, compound doors open, enemy production stops refilling the guard.
9. 17:00–20:00 — Compound assault through the west gate: two advanced_defences, four towers, two heavy tanks. Siege guns again from outside range. Engineer capture of the command building is faster than destroying 1500 HP.
10. ~21:00 — Command building taken: MSG_09, win. Optional: blueprint crate at (100, 72) (evidence_F2-M09).

## K. After the mission

- **Carry over**: Campaign flag `completed_F2-M09`. Evidence flag `evidence_F2-M09` if OBJ_BATTERY_PLANS was recovered; in F2-M10 it reveals the automated_gun ranges on the minimap from the start. Flag `barrage_suffered` = yes if TR_08 fired (changes one Sava line in the F2-M10 briefing). Surviving units do not carry over.
- **Choice**: Continue to F2-M10.
- **Test scenario**:
  1. TR_01 fires once; attacking OBJ_UPLINK at the start does no damage; TR_15 hint shows when a unit climbs the ridge.
  2. Destroy OBJ_GENERATOR_2 first: its two towers stop firing; MSG_04 plays once regardless of which generator died first.
  3. Destroy all three: MSG_05, countdown 5:00 visible, T_UPLINK_DEFENCE appears at the ridge foot; uplink now takes damage.
  4. Let the countdown expire: OBJ_LOCAL_GENERATOR turns on, MSG_07, four shells land within 6 cells of (20, 108) every 20 s; objective D appears.
  5. During the barrage destroy OBJ_UPLINK: barrage stops, doors open, MSG_08; alternatively destroy OBJ_LOCAL_GENERATOR: barrage stops, doors stay sealed.
  6. Attack OBJ_COMMAND_BUILDING while the uplink is alive: no damage; after the uplink dies: damage and capture possible.
  7. Destroy the uplink at 0:05 remaining: TR_08 never fires; MSG_06 played earlier.
  8. Count waves: exactly 7 at 4:00 intervals, odd via the east underpass, even via the west; waves 3+ include a heavy_tank; no wave after the command building is gone.
  9. Lose construction_center, factory and barracks under the barrage: TR_12 loss with MSG_10.
  10. Pick up OBJ_BATTERY_PLANS: MSG_11 once, `evidence_F2-M09` set.

## Notes

This is an original real-time-strategy campaign inspired by the broad structure of classic 1990s RTS campaigns: faction-specific operations, base-building, commando missions, escorts, timed defence, sabotage, and escalating counterattacks. It does not reuse Red Alert characters, dialogue, mission scripts, or named story events.
