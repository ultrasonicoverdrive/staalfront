## B. Story

- **Situation**: Both armies converge on an abandoned coordination hub where the forged orders originated. Secure the evidence vault and prevent its destruction.
- **Briefing**: `briefings/F2-M10.fountain`
- **Mid-mission twist**: The hub's automated defence system treats every faction as hostile; the player must disable it before either army can enter.
- **Story beat**: The campaign closes on proof of a deliberate false-flag operation, leaving a choice about what to reveal.

- **Ending (win)**: The vault stands, its demolition charges disarmed, and the files inside prove that one private network wrote the orders that started the war for both sides. Sava and Lenka stand over the records as the Meridian survivors withdraw. The choice is now theirs: publish everything and end two governments' pretence, or hold it back and use it. The campaign ends on that decision, and the flag `reveal_F2` records it.
- **Ending (loss)**: The vault detonates or the forward base falls, and the originals of the forged orders burn with the hub. Each side keeps its own version of who fired first. The broker's network survives to do it again.

## C. Map

- **Map**: new
- **Size and terrain**: Large, 128×128 cells. Black Basin, dawn. The abandoned coordination hub sits at the centre: a circular concrete ring 40 cells across with three gated entrances (north, east, south) and a dry drainage culvert under the south-west wall. Inside the ring: the vault (a squat archive building) at the exact centre, the defence control bunker in the north-west quadrant, a mail room in the south-east quadrant, and ruined relay masts. Outside the ring: ruined barracks and rail sidings to the east and south, foundry slag to the north, a wide open plain to the west where the Meridian column will arrive. Player base in the south-east corner, with an ore field.

Inside: the vault at the centre (heavy doors, antenna stubs), the control bunker at ten o'clock, the mail room at four o'clock. Bottom-right corner: the player base. Left edge: an empty plain with a road entering mid-height, marked "Meridian 12:00". Ruins and sidings between the base and the east gate.

## J. Flow in words

1. 0:00 — Briefing. Full base, full roster, 6000 credits, Meridian expected at 12:00. The hub wall is on the minimap; its guns are not.
2. 0:00–4:00 — Player builds up (second factory, more siege guns, bunkers on the west side of the base) and scouts. First sight of a gun: MSG_03 reveals the six guns and the existence of the control bunker. Scouts find the culvert (MSG_13).
3. 4:00–8:00 — Two ways in. Stealth: engineers and a medic walk the culvert (infantry only; about 130 cells from the base, 3:09 on foot), reach the control bunker and capture it without a shot; the ring goes dark (TR_09) and the countdown starts (TR_04) with no losses. Force: siege_artillery from P_SIEGE_STAND_E kills OBJ_AGUN_E1/E2 from range 10; the countdown starts on the first kill.
4. ~8:00 — Countdown 8:00 (MSG_04/05). The player has until ~16:00 to get an engineer into the vault; Meridian arrives at 12:00. A player who moved fast can secure the vault before Meridian is even on the map.
5. 12:00 — T_MERIDIAN_MAIN (20 units plus passengers) crosses the west edge (MSG_06) and reaches the gates about 1:10 later (68 cells at the siege pace of 1.0). If the ring is still live, it shells the north and south guns from range and loses units to them; if the ring is dark, it drives straight for the vault with engineers in transports.
6. 12:00–16:00 — Hub fight. Veyran bunkers and heavy_gun_teams inside the ring hold the gates; siege guns on the sidings shell the Meridian approach. T_MERIDIAN_ENGINEERS race for the vault (62 cells ÷ 2.5 = 25 s once the ring is dark); killing their transport is the priority.
7. ~15:00 — Engineer secures the vault (TR_10): countdown stops, vault invulnerable, MSG_10. Warnings at 4:00 and 1:30 if the player is late.
8. 14:30, 17:00, 19:30, 22:00 — four follow-up Meridian waves (16 each) through the open gates and then on to the player base; advanced_defences and the two starting heavy tanks hold Z_START. Losing the forward base is still a loss after the vault is secured until TR_11 fires, so TR_11 is deliberately only 5 s after both conditions.
9. 20:00–24:00 — Mop up or hold; optional mail-room files (evidence_F2-M10).
10. ~25:00 — End choice dialog: reveal or withhold. Either answer wins; `reveal_F2` is written and the epilogue follows.

## K. After the mission

- **Carry over**: Campaign flag `completed_F2-M10`; campaign flag `reveal_F2` = yes/no from the end choice (selects the Veyran epilogue text). Evidence flag `evidence_F2-M10` if the mail-room files were recovered (adds the ministers' names to the epilogue). No further missions.
- **Choice**: End of the Veyran campaign. The reveal/withhold choice is made inside the mission (TR_11), never blocks the win, and is shown in the campaign summary screen.
- **Test scenario**:
  1. TR_01 fires once; Meridian timer counts from 12:00; guns are hidden unless `evidence_F2-M09` is set.
  2. Drive a light_tank to 8 cells from OBJ_AGUN_E1: it is fired on; at 9 cells it is not. A Meridian unit in the same place is also fired on.
  3. Kill OBJ_AGUN_E1 with siege_artillery: `timer_selfdestruct` starts at 8:00, MSG_04 then MSG_05 once; killing a second gun does not restart it.
  4. Capture OBJ_DEFENCE_CONTROL via the culvert without destroying any gun: all six guns turn Player-coloured and stop firing; the countdown starts from this event; MSG_09.
  5. Let the countdown expire: vault explodes, MSG_11, loss. Enter the vault with an engineer at 0:10: countdown stops, vault invulnerable, Meridian siege fire does no damage to it.
  6. Secure the vault but leave the ring live: no win; capture the bunker: TR_11 fires after 5 s with the choice dialog; pick "Withhold": `reveal_F2` = no, win.
  7. At 12:00 T_MERIDIAN_MAIN appears at the west edge; with the ring live its siege guns engage the north and south gate guns and the guns fire back.
  8. Let a Meridian engineer enter the vault before the player: TR_12 loss.
  9. Lose OBJ_FORWARD_BASE at 20:00 with the vault secured but the bunker not captured: TR_13 loss.
  10. Count follow-up waves: exactly 4, 150 s apart, starting 150 s after the main column.
  11. Pick up OBJ_MAIL_ROOM_FILES: MSG_14 once, `evidence_F2-M10` set.

## Notes

This is an original real-time-strategy campaign inspired by the broad structure of classic 1990s RTS campaigns: faction-specific operations, base-building, commando missions, escorts, timed defence, sabotage, and escalating counterattacks. It does not reuse Red Alert characters, dialogue, mission scripts, or named story events.
