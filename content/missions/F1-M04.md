## B. Story

- **Situation**: A rail switchyard controls the only route for reinforcements. Hold it until demolition teams can disable the enemy rail spur.
- **Briefing**: `briefings/F1-M04.fountain`
- **Mid-mission twist**: Enemy engineers attempt to repair a damaged crossing, opening a second attack lane.
- **Story beat**: The front stabilises, but the enemy begins adapting to the player's tactics.

- **Ending (win)**: The spur is cut at 18:00 and the switchyard is still in Compact hands. The front north of the ridge stabilises for the first time since the relay went dark. But Mira's notes show the enemy shifted lanes and targets each time the player's defences settled, including a bridge repair that no Veyran unit in this sector was equipped for. The enemy is learning the Compact's habits, or someone is teaching them.
- **Ending (loss)**: The demolition crew is killed or the switchyard falls before 18:00. Enemy reinforcements arrive by rail at the rate of a company an hour, and the forward positions the convoy supplied in F1-M03 are abandoned within a day.

## C. Map

- **Map**: new
- **Size and terrain**: Medium, 96×96 cells; rail switchyard at centre, two approach roads, observation hill to the north-east.
- **Sketch**: create `missions/F1-M04-map.png` during map production.
- **Sketch description**: The switchyard sits at map centre: six parallel rail tracks running north–south, a signal box, a goods shed and a water crane, inside a loose fence. The player's base pad is on the west side of the yard. An ore field lies just south-west of the base. The north road enters from the north edge and narrows between two rail embankments 14 cells north of the yard (the north choke). A canal runs east–west across the southern third of the map; the south road crosses it on a single-span bridge 16 cells south of the yard, and the bridge deck is collapsed at mission start. South of the canal the road runs off the south edge. The enemy rail spur leaves the yard to the north-east and ends at a buffer 20 cells away in a cutting, where the demolition crew works. An observation hill with a ruined signal tower stands in the north-east corner, overlooking both the spur and the north road.

## J. Flow in words

1. 0:00 — Briefing, MSG_01/02, timer 18:00 shown. Base is pre-built with 4000 credits; MSG_03 at 0:15 suggests towers at the north choke. Player builds 2–3 defence_tower at Z_NORTH_CHOKE and a second power_plant.
2. 2:00 — Wave 1 (8 units) down the north road, MSG_04. Player learns rockets behind towers and the repair truck behind tanks.
3. 4:30 — Wave 2 (10 units) with rocket troopers targeting the towers. Player repairs with the construction_yard and adds towers; scout sent to P_OBS_TOWER reveals the north road for early warning.
4. 7:00 — Wave 3 (10 units), north only unless the bridge is already open.
5. 8:00 — MSG_05 near the halfway mark: enemy stops hitting towers head-on.
6. 9:00 (`timer_main` 9:00) — Twist: T_BRIDGE_CREW arrives on the south bank, MSG_06/07, objective C. The bridge opens after ~25 s of uninterrupted repair. Player decides: send the tanks south to kill engineers, shoot the bridge down (100 HP), or accept a second lane and tower the south approach.
7. 9:30 — Wave 4 (12 units); split north/south if `bridge_open` already. The refinery on the south side of the base is the south half's target.
8. 12:00 and 14:30 — Waves 5 and 6 (12 units each), split if the bridge is open. If the player destroyed the bridge early, T_BRIDGE_CREW_2 shows up with a tank escort and tries again; the player must now watch the south permanently.
9. 15:00 (`timer_main` 3:00) — MSG_09; T_SPUR_RAID (6 units) enters from the east edge straight for the demolition crew. Player must keep a tank pair or tower near Z_SPUR.
10. 17:00 — Wave 7 (12 units) ignores towers and drives for the signal box on both lanes.
11. 18:00 — TR_12: spur explodes, MSG_10/11, win.
12. Loss branches: 6 enemies around the signal box for 30 s (TR_11); crew killed (TR_13); construction_yard lost early (TR_14).

## K. After the mission

- **Carry over**: Campaign flag `completed_F1-M04` and `evidence_m04_adaptation` (set on win; used in the F1-M05 briefing line about the enemy reading Compact tactics). No unit carry-over.
- **Choice**: Continue to F1-M05.
- **Test scenario**:
  1. TR_01 fires once; `timer_main` shows 18:00 and counts down; defence_tower is in the build menu, heavy_tank and advanced_defences are not.
  2. Wave 1 spawns at 2:00 at P_NORTH_ENTRY with exactly 6 rifleman + 2 light_tank; wave 2 at 4:30; wave 7 at 17:00; no wave 8.
  3. At `timer_main` 9:00 T_BRIDGE_CREW spawns at P_SOUTH_ENTRY; with 3 engineers adjacent the bridge reaches 250 HP after 25 s, `bridge_open` = yes, MSG_08, and units can cross. Kill one engineer: repair slows to 4 HP/s.
  4. Shoot the bridge to 0 HP before the crew arrives: `bridge_rubble` = yes, T_BRIDGE_CREW_2 arrives 60 s later; with all three engineers the bridge is passable again after 60 s.
  5. With `bridge_open` = yes at 9:30, wave 4 spawns half at P_NORTH_ENTRY and half at P_SOUTH_ENTRY; with `bridge_open` = no, all 12 at P_NORTH_ENTRY.
  6. At `timer_main` 3:00 T_SPUR_RAID spawns at P_EAST_EDGE and targets `OBJ_DEMO_CREW` before any other unit.
  7. Kill both `OBJ_DEMO_1` and `OBJ_DEMO_2`: TR_13 loss immediately; kill one: no loss and win still possible.
  8. Park 6 Enemy 1 units within 6 cells of P_SIGNAL_BOX for 29 s then remove one: no loss; 30 s: TR_11 loss.
  9. Reach 0:00 with the yard held: `OBJ_RAIL_SPUR` is destroyed, MSG_10 and MSG_11 play, win screen; no win fires at 0:01 before the spur explosion.

## Notes

This is an original real-time-strategy campaign inspired by the broad structure of classic 1990s RTS campaigns: faction-specific operations, base-building, commando missions, escorts, timed defence, sabotage, and escalating counterattacks. It does not reuse Red Alert characters, dialogue, mission scripts, or named story events.
