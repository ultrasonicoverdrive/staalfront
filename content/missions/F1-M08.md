## B. Story

- **Situation**: A field army must hold a plateau until allied units arrive at dawn. The plateau has limited resources and several approach routes.
- **Briefing**: `briefings/F1-M08.fountain`
- **Mid-mission twist**: The first relief signal is a trap; the true allied column arrives from the western pass later.
- **Story beat**: Trust and verification become central to the war.

- **Ending (win)**: Dawn comes up over a plateau still in Meridian hands, with the real relief column rolling up through the western pass. The false column's wrecks on the east road carry Meridian paint over Veyran steel, and their radio set was tuned to a frequency neither army uses. Mira writes the first standing order of the campaign: no signal is accepted without a challenge.
- **Ending (loss)**: The plateau falls before dawn. Whether it fell to the night assaults or to the column that was let through the wire, the result is the same: the western pass is closed and the relief column turns back.

## J. Flow in words

1. **0:00** Briefing; 22-minute clock. Small base on the plateau, small ore field inside the wire, a richer one in the hollow below the north rim.
2. **1:00–2:30** T_HARASS scouts and riflemen snipe at harvesters. Player builds a second refinery, decides whether to work the hollow (more income, exposed to the north lane).
3. **2:30** Wave 1 (north lane, 12). Player learns the lanes; towers cover north and east, the south ramp is open.
4. **5:00, 7:30, 10:00** Waves 2–4 (east, south, north). Economy funds a heavy_tank or two and a third tower at the south ramp. Optional raid on the enemy camp to kill a factory and thin later waves.
5. **11:00** MSG_06. A convoy in Meridian colours appears on the east road calling itself the relief. Mira challenges and gets no valid reply (MSG_03). Player must decide: let it climb or fire first.
6. **11:00–13:00** If let in, the column turns in the base centre and its engineers go for the construction center (TR_08, MSG_04). If fired on, it turns at the ramp. Either way the player fights 16 units on the plateau and must not chase down the east road into wave 5.
7. **12:00, 14:00** Waves 5–6 (east, south) at 120 s intervals.
8. **14:00** T_PASS_BLOCK enters the western gorge and digs in at P_PASS_HOLD (MSG_05). Player must send a force down the pass before 20:00 or the real relief gets stuck.
9. **16:00, 18:00** Waves 7–8; wave 8 brings 2 heavy_tank from two lanes at once.
10. **20:00** The true relief column enters the gorge with the correct challenge (MSG_07). If the pass is clear it is on the plateau by about 21:00; if blocked, it fights through and may arrive after dawn.
11. **22:00** Dawn. Win when relief has reached the plateau and the construction center stands. If the relief is still in the pass at 22:00, TR_15 fires as soon as it arrives.
12. **End** The false column's wrecks carry fresh Meridian paint and a radio tuned to neither army's net. Verification becomes doctrine.

## K. After the mission

- **Carry over**: Campaign flag `completed_F1-M08`; flag `m08_false_column_held_at_ramp` if TR_08 fired by player attack before any column unit entered Z_PLATEAU_CORE (next briefing praises the challenge discipline); flag `m08_factory_raid` if `factories_down` >= 1.
- **Evidence**: `evidence_m08` set on win if at least one supply_truck of T_FALSE_RELIEF was destroyed on the plateau (its radio set is recovered). Adds a line to the F1-M09 briefing about a frequency neither army uses.
- **Choice**: Continue to F1-M09.
- **Test scenario**:
  1. TR_01 fires once; `timer_dawn` shows 22:00 and counts down; `timer_wave` releases wave 1 at 2:30.
  2. Waves 1–8 alternate lanes as listed; after one enemy factory is destroyed, the next wave is 2 units smaller, never below 8.
  3. T_FALSE_RELIEF appears at 11:00 in player colours, is not auto-targeted, and turns hostile on entering Z_PLATEAU_CORE, on being attacked, or 60 s after reaching P_EAST_RAMP; TR_08 fires only once.
  4. An Eastern Column engineer reaching OBJ_CONSTRUCTION_CENTER captures it and TR_11 fires (loss).
  5. With T_PASS_BLOCK alive in Z_WEST_PASS at 20:00, T_TRUE_RELIEF still spawns, fights in the gorge, and TR_14 fires only when a relief unit reaches Z_PLATEAU; TR_15 does not fire at 22:00 in that case and wins when the relief arrives.
  6. With the pass clear, relief reaches Z_PLATEAU_CORE before 22:00 and TR_15 wins exactly at `timer_dawn` = 0.
  7. Destroying the enemy construction_center and both factories does not win the mission on its own.
  8. heavy_tank and radar are buildable from the start; advanced_defences is not.
