## B. Story

- **Situation**: Reconnaissance suggests a fortified enemy command post, but much of the visible force is a decoy screen.
- **Briefing**: `briefings/F1-M05.fountain`
- **Mid-mission twist**: Destroying the decoy depot reveals a lightly defended real supply office elsewhere on the map.
- **Story beat**: The enemy is using theatre and false intelligence to stretch defenders thin.

- **Ending (win)**: The real supply office at the grain exchange is destroyed. The decoy fort cost the Compact nothing but time, which was exactly the point: every shell aimed at painted guns was a shell not aimed at the office that moved the enemy's fuel. If the ledger was recovered, it shows the fort's "garrison" was ordered as timber and canvas from a civilian supplier whose invoice is not addressed to any Veyran unit. The enemy's theatre has a stage manager, and it is not the enemy.
- **Ending (loss)**: The strike force is spent against the fort or the markings are blown before the office is found. The grain exchange keeps feeding the front, the next Compact reconnaissance flight photographs an even bigger fortress, and the Compact command diverts another battalion to watch it.

## C. Map

- **Map**: new
- **Size and terrain**: Medium, 96×80 cells; conspicuous decoy fort in the north, real supply office in the south-east, scattered neutral sheds.
- **Sketch**: create `missions/F1-M05-map.png` during map production.
- **Sketch description**: Player's forward post is in the south-west corner on dry farmland. A straight road runs north from it to the decoy fort, which fills the north-centre of the map: earth walls, a visible ring of gun emplacements, tent rows, and a fuel depot in the middle. A second, less obvious road runs east from the player's post along the south edge through scattered neutral sheds and grain stores to the old grain exchange in the south-east corner: a brick hall with a loading yard, one gate on the west side, a rifle picket at the gate. Between the two roads, open fields with hedgerows. Enemy pickets (two-man rifle posts) sit on both roads at the halfway points. North-east corner: a tent camp where the real guard force waits.

## J. Flow in words

1. 0:00 — Briefing, MSG_01–03. Player has a post, 2000 credits, a small strike force and two marked tanks. MSG_04 at 0:30 points at decoy_emplacement (150 credits each).
2. 1:00–2:00 — Player places 3–4 decoy_emplacement along Z_NORTH_ROAD in front of the post. First T_PROBE (5 units, D2 size) at 2:00 shoots the decoys instead of the barracks; riflemen and rockets kill it.
3. 2:00–5:00 — Scouts go out. The north scout reaches the fort (TR_05, MSG_05: painted guns). The south scout sneaks through Z_SHEDS and may spot P_OFFICE directly (TR_07, quiet reveal). Probes keep coming every 2:00 and keep eating decoys.
4. 5:00–7:00 — Decision point. The loud route: rockets and riflemen go through P_FORT_GATE past 2 pickets and 4 guards to blow `OBJ_DECOY_DEPOT`. The quiet route: skip the fort and go straight for the office if the scout found it.
5. 7:00 — Twist (loud route): TR_06, MSG_06/07, Z_GRAIN_EXCHANGE and Z_GUARD_CAMP revealed, `timer_relocate` 1:30 starts, T_REAL_GUARD (8 units) will move to the office.
6. 7:00–9:00 — `OBJ_CAPTURED_TANKS` drive down Z_SOUTH_ROAD past T_PICKET_SOUTH and T_PICKET_EXCHANGE unchallenged (markings intact). Infantry follow at a distance of more than 4 cells from the tanks or the pickets spot the trick.
7. 9:00–11:00 — Tanks sit next to `OBJ_SUPPLY_OFFICE` and open fire: TR_04, MSG_08, markings blown, but the office (800 HP) is already taking two cannons at point-blank. Infantry clear the exchange picket and an engineer or rifleman walks onto `OBJ_LEDGER` (TR_10, MSG_10).
8. 11:00–13:00 — T_REAL_GUARD arrives; player finishes the office under pressure or falls back with the ledger and returns with rockets.
9. 13:00–14:00 — TR_11: office destroyed, MSG_09, win.
10. Loss branch: strike force spent on the fort with no credits left (TR_12), or the post lost to probes while the force is away (TR_13).

## K. After the mission

- **Carry over**: Campaign flag `completed_F1-M05`, `evidence_m05_ledger` (optional, set by TR_10; adds the civilian-supplier line to the F1-M06 briefing). No unit carry-over.
- **Choice**: Continue to F1-M06.
- **Test scenario**:
  1. TR_01 fires once; decoy_emplacement is in the build menu at 150 credits; defence_tower is also available; amphibious_transport and sniper are not.
  2. Place a decoy_emplacement 8 cells north of the barracks: the first T_PROBE at 2:00 shoots the decoy before any other structure.
  3. Drive `OBJ_CAPT_1` alone past P_SOUTH_PICKET and P_EXCHANGE_GATE: no enemy fires on it and no alert is raised. Walk a rifleman 3 cells behind it in sight of the picket: TR_04 fires, MSG_08, picket opens fire on both.
  4. Fire `OBJ_CAPT_2` at any target: `markings_blown` = yes for both tanks within the same tick.
  5. Destroy `OBJ_DECOY_DEPOT`: radius-3 explosion, MSG_06 and MSG_07 once, Z_GRAIN_EXCHANGE revealed, objective B shown, `timer_relocate` runs 1:30 and T_REAL_GUARD leaves P_GUARD_CAMP when it expires.
  6. Scout sights P_OFFICE before the depot is destroyed: TR_07 reveals the exchange without a message; destroying the depot later still plays MSG_06/07 and starts `timer_relocate`.
  7. Blow the markings with `office_found` = yes and T_REAL_GUARD not yet released: T_REAL_GUARD leaves immediately (TR_09).
  8. Destroy `OBJ_SUPPLY_OFFICE` without touching the fort: win; `depot_down` stays no and no loss or penalty fires.
  9. Walk infantry onto `OBJ_LEDGER`: `evidence_m05_ledger` = yes and MSG_10 once; pick it up after the office is destroyed in the same 5 s: MSG_10 before MSG_09.
  10. Send all 5 probes: a sixth never spawns; `timer_probe` stops after the fifth release.

## Notes

This is an original real-time-strategy campaign inspired by the broad structure of classic 1990s RTS campaigns: faction-specific operations, base-building, commando missions, escorts, timed defence, sabotage, and escalating counterattacks. It does not reuse Red Alert characters, dialogue, mission scripts, or named story events.
