## B. Story

- **Situation**: A communications relay is broadcasting false orders that are sending friendly units into ambushes. Reach the relay and disable it without raising the alarm.
- **Briefing**: `briefings/F1-M02.fountain`
- **Mid-mission twist**: A civilian technician reveals a second transmitter hidden in the service yard.
- **Story beat**: The opposing faction is manipulating the front through misinformation.

- **Ending (win)**: Both transmitters are silent and the team is out through the drainage channel. The technician's statement and the frequency log show the "relay" was never Veyran army equipment: it was installed by a civilian contractor three weeks ago, with orders written on Compact letterhead. The front is being moved by someone who can forge both sides' paperwork.
- **Ending (loss)**: The team is destroyed inside the compound, or the civilian technicians are killed. The relay keeps broadcasting, two more patrols walk into ambushes before dawn, and the Compact loses its only lead on where the false orders come from.

## C. Map

- **Map**: new
- **Size and terrain**: Small, 64×48 cells; relay compound in the east, drainage channel along the south, service path through scrub.
- **Sketch**: create `missions/F1-M02-map.png` during map production.
- **Sketch description**: Player starts in the west edge, middle height, in a tree line. A concrete drainage channel runs along the whole southern edge, two cells wide and sunk one level (units inside are hidden from the road). A gravel road runs from the north edge down to the relay compound in the east-centre: a fenced square with the main mast in the middle, a guard hut at the west gate and a sandbag post at the north gate. North of the compound, across the road, is a service yard with a water tower, a workshop and three parked trucks. Between the player start and the compound lies scrub with ruin cells (old farm walls) in a rough line, the "service path". A footbridge over the channel at the compound's south fence is the exit point.

## J. Flow in words

1. 0:00 — Briefing, MSG_01/02. Eight units in the tree line, no base. Objectives A and C shown.
2. 0:30–3:00 — Scouts lead along Z_SERVICE_PATH (MSG_03 on entry). The player learns to park a scout on a ruin cell to watch T_COMPOUND_PATROL's loop and that Z_CHANNEL is never watched.
3. 3:00–5:00 — First sighting is likely at the west gate; `alarm_level` 1, MSG_04. Riflemen kill T_GATE_WEST quickly while the patrol is at the north gate.
4. 5:00–7:00 — Engineer places the charge on `OBJ_MAST`; TR_06, MSG_06 ("still on the air"). Alarm goes to 2 if it was at 1: T_RESPONSE (7 units, D2 size) musters for 90 s and then needs 29 s for the 20-cell north road (20 ÷ 0.7), so it is 2:00 away.
5. 7:00–8:30 — A scout or rifleman reaches the technicians at P_WORKSHOP; TR_07, MSG_07 reveals `OBJ_HIDDEN_SET` under the water tower, objective B shown.
6. 8:30–10:30 — T_YARD_GUARD must be removed without shooting into the technicians (they flee from gunfire). The fuel_depot is a tempting but risky shortcut.
7. 10:30–12:00 — Second charge on `OBJ_HIDDEN_SET`; TR_09, MSG_08, Z_EXIT revealed, objective D shown. T_RESPONSE is now sweeping the compound.
8. 12:00–14:00 — Team drops into Z_CHANNEL and moves to the footbridge, avoiding the sweep.
9. 14:00–15:00 — TR_10: engineer at P_EXIT, win, `evidence_m02_frequency` set.
10. Loss branches: both engineers lost (TR_13); two technicians killed (TR_12); team wiped (TR_14).

## K. After the mission

- **Carry over**: Campaign flag `completed_F1-M02` and `evidence_m02_frequency` (set on win; names the contractor frequency in the F1-M03 briefing). No unit carry-over.
- **Choice**: Continue to F1-M03.
- **Test scenario**:
  1. TR_01 fires once; player has 2 scout, 2 engineer, 4 rifleman, no construction_center, 0 credits, no build menu.
  2. Park a scout on a ruin cell in Z_SERVICE_PATH within sight 5 of T_COMPOUND_PATROL: `alarm_level` stays 0. Move it: `alarm_level` becomes 1 after 2 s, MSG_04 once.
  3. Two sightings 5 s apart raise `alarm_level` only to 1; a third sighting 20 s later raises it to 2 and TR_05 releases exactly 6 rifleman + 1 light_tank at P_NORTH_ENTRY.
  4. Destroy `OBJ_MAST` with no prior sighting: `alarm_level` becomes 1, not 2; MSG_06 plays once.
  5. Walk a rifleman within 3 cells of `OBJ_LEAD_TECH` before the mast is down: TR_07 fires and `OBJ_HIDDEN_SET` becomes visible and targetable.
  6. Kill `OBJ_LEAD_TECH` first, then destroy the mast: TR_08 fires, Z_SERVICE_YARD revealed without MSG_07, and TR_12 fires only if a second technician dies.
  7. Destroy `OBJ_HIDDEN_SET` before `OBJ_MAST`: `hidden_down` not set until the mast is also down; MSG_08 plays after the second kill.
  8. Reach Z_EXIT with only riflemen while both engineers are dead: TR_13 has already fired a loss; reach it with one engineer alive: win.
  9. Trigger the fuel_depot blast that kills 2 technicians: TR_12 loss, MSG_10.

## Notes

This is an original real-time-strategy campaign inspired by the broad structure of classic 1990s RTS campaigns: faction-specific operations, base-building, commando missions, escorts, timed defence, sabotage, and escalating counterattacks. It does not reuse Red Alert characters, dialogue, mission scripts, or named story events.

**Timing check** (speeds from `units.md`; distance in cells ÷ speed = time; retuned 17-10-2026, Tijmen approved):
- T_COMPOUND_PATROL: P_WEST_GATE → P_MAST → P_NORTH_GATE → P_MAST → P_WEST_GATE is 7 + 6 + 6 + 7 = 26 cells ÷ 0.7 = 37 s, plus 2 × 8 s pause = 53 s. Written as a 55 s loop (was "40 s loop").
- T_RESPONSE: Z_NORTH_ROAD is 20 cells ÷ 0.7 = 29 s. A direct release would arrive in half a minute, so the team now musters for 90 s first, which keeps the "2:00 away" of the flow.
- Z_SERVICE_PATH (30 cells): scout 23 s, rifleman 43 s. `timer_response` (240 s) stays the fallback sweep.
- The 15 min target is stealth waiting, not walking. No target change.
