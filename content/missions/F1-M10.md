## B. Story

- **Situation**: The enemy's field command has concentrated its remaining strength around a regional control centre. Capture the centre's data archive and stop the launch of a final offensive.
- **Briefing**: `briefings/F1-M10.fountain`
- **Mid-mission twist**: The command centre begins an automated evacuation protocol; the player must secure the archive before it is erased.
- **Story beat**: The first campaign ends with proof that a third party helped ignite the conflict.

- **Ending (win)**: The control centre falls with its archive intact. The deployment orders that started the war carry a routing header from a private logistics-and-intelligence network, and the same header is on the forged orders Meridian received in the first week. The records show that both armies were manipulated into believing the other had struck first; they do not by themselves establish the identity of every attacker. Elian sends authenticated extracts through military channels to commands on both sides, requesting verification and a halt to escalation. The regional offensive is stopped, but this is not yet public disclosure of the network's complete files. The original keys, templates and correspondence remain at a separate coordination hub. Someone sold both armies the war, and for the first time in weeks the horizon is quiet.
- **Ending (loss)**: The archive wipes itself or burns, or the final offensive breaks the forward base. The war ends somewhere, eventually, but without the records nobody can say who began it, and the network that did is free to do it again.

## J. Flow in words

1. **0:00** Briefing. Full roster. Base on the terrace with 6000 credits and three ore fields, the third outside the towers. A 15:00 offensive clock appears at 0:20 with the marshalling yard marked.
2. **0:20–3:00** Scouts go up the canal path and into the foundry ruins. Player expands economy and builds siege_artillery and heavy_tank. Choice: pre-empt the yard via the canal path, or fortify the south side of the base and let the offensive come.
3. **3:00, 6:00, 9:00, 12:00** T_PROBE (18) down the west road, the outer ore field first. Advanced_defences and heavy_tank hold the terrace edge.
4. **5:00–12:00** Canal route: 3–4 siege_artillery at P_CANAL_OVERLOOK shell factory A from 10 cells; T_YARD_RESPONSE comes out and must be held by a tank screen. The fuel depots are worth shelling first: each takes 3 cells of parked armour with it.
5. **~13:00** If both yard factories die, the clock stops and objective B completes (TR_09, TR_10); the parked armour stays parked. If not, at **15:00** the column (up to 18) moves through the centre and out the south gate (MSG_06). It reaches the base at about 17:30 (153 cells to the base, 139 s for the heavy tanks; the artillery halts at range 10 after 2:23); the player meets it with everything.
6. **12:00–17:00** Main force breaks the west gate: artillery outranges the two advanced_defences, heavy_tank screens, engineers kept back in the light_transport.
7. **~17:00** First player unit onto the inner platform. Twist: 6:00 wipe clock (MSG_04), T_INNER_GUARD holds the only inner gate.
8. **17:00–20:00** Player chooses: punch through the inner gate with armour and run an engineer 7 cells to the data room door (15 s capture), or shell the generator at P_PROTOCOL_GENERATOR from outside the inner wall to stop the clock, then take the gate at leisure. Shelling must avoid the archive 8 cells away.
9. **20:00** MSG_05 at 3:00 remaining if the clock is still running.
10. **~22:00** Engineer captures the data room: objective A and C complete, MSG_07.
11. **~22:00** As soon as A and B are both complete, TR_12 wins immediately. If the offensive was already cancelled or destroyed, capture around 22:00 ends the mission then; there is no compulsory wait until 24:00. Optional relay or construction_center destruction must happen before the final required objective is completed.
12. **End** The routing header on the deployment orders matches the forged Meridian orders from the first week: a private network sold both sides the war. Campaign closes.

## Notes

All content is original: no characters, dialogue, missions or scenes are taken from existing games. The mission structure follows common genre patterns (base-building, commando, escort, timed defence, sabotage).

**Objective C exception (27-10-2026):** C is completed by whichever comes first: TR_07 (generator destroyed while C is shown), TR_05 (the player enters after the power was already cut) or TR_11 (archive captured). This is a deliberate exception to CONVENTIONS rule 10, because the player can stop the wipe in three ways.
