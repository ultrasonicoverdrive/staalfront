## B. Story

- **Situation**: An enemy research station is tracking every friendly movement along the coast. A compact team must disable its sensor array while the main force draws attention.
- **Briefing**: `briefings/F1-M07.fountain`
- **Mid-mission twist**: The array is protected by a backup generator in a separate compound.
- **Story beat**: The enemy's information advantage can be broken.

- **Ending (win)**: The station's last mast folds into the surf and the generator compound burns behind it. For the first time since the war began, Meridian patrols move without being expected. Mira takes the station's tracking logs with her: the targets it was watching include villages on the Veyran side as well.
- **Ending (loss)**: The commando team is lost in the drainage cut or inside the walls, and the array returns to full power within the hour. Every convoy on the coast road is still being watched, and the diversion force is withdrawn with nothing to show for its dead.

## J. Flow in words

1. **0:00** Briefing. Seven commandos in the south-west corner; the diversion force is visible under the trees in the north-west. No base, no money.
2. **0:15** Elian explains the signal: a scout on the ridge marker launches the diversion. Player decides on timing.
3. **1:00–4:00** Team moves up the drainage cut, scouts leading. T_ROAD_PATROL is visible on the service road through scout sight. Sniper kills the 2 culvert guards from range 8 without raising alarm if no tower sees the shooter.
4. **~4:00** Player sends one scout west-north to Z_SIGNAL_RIDGE (52 cells ÷ 1.3, about 40 s). Diversion attacks the north gate; the gate towers and half the mast guards pull north (MSG_04).
5. **5:00–9:00** Engineers slip through the culvert. Sniper picks off remaining mast guards one at a time. Charges placed on masts A, B, C (charge timer 10 s each). `alarm_level` creeps up with each mast.
6. **~9:30** Third mast down. Twist: generator compound revealed, 90-second restart timer, T_COMPOUND_RELIEF (4 riflemen, 2 tanks) heads up the service road. Diversion survivors can be left to screen the gate.
7. **9:30–11:00** From the last mast to the generator is about 40 cells (57 s at 0.7) plus 10 s for the charge, so a team that walks straight there is inside the 90 s on paper. In practice it must pass T_ROAD_PATROL, four riflemen and a tower, and T_COMPOUND_RELIEF (it leaves the barracks at once and covers the 22 cells to the generator in 31 s), so the timer nearly always expires and OBJ_ARRAY_CONTROL raises a replacement mast at P_MAST_A. The player learns the sequence: generator first, then the last mast, or leave an engineer with a charge ready beside the mast.
8. **11:00–14:00** Sweeps come down the drainage cut every 150 s (max 3). Team moves north along the service road, uses the ruin cells beside it to let T_COMPOUND_RELIEF pass, sniper thins the compound guard, engineer charges the generator.
9. **~14:30** Generator down. Objective B complete; restart timer stops.
10. **14:30–16:00** Return to the station (40 cells, 57 s) and charge the rebuilt mast; the gate guard is depleted by the diversion.
11. **~16:30** Last mast down with the generator dead: win. MSG_07.
12. **End** Mira carries the tracking logs out: the station was watching both sides' villages.

## Notes

This is an original real-time-strategy campaign inspired by the broad structure of classic 1990s RTS campaigns: faction-specific operations, base-building, commando missions, escorts, timed defence, sabotage, and escalating counterattacks. It does not reuse Red Alert characters, dialogue, mission scripts, or named story events.

**Timing check** (speeds from `units.md`; distance in cells ÷ speed = time; retuned 17-10-2026, Tijmen approved):
- Scout to Z_SIGNAL_RIDGE: P_COMMANDO_START (7, 72) → P_SIGNAL_FLARE (29, 25) is 52 cells ÷ 1.3 = 40 s (the flow said about 90 s). Drainage cut (10, 68) → (50, 58): 41 cells ÷ 0.7 = 59 s. T_SWEEP: P_SWEEP_ENTRY → P_COMMANDO_START 43 cells = 61 s.
- Last mast → generator: about 40 cells ÷ 0.7 = 57 s plus 10 s for the charge, against `timer_restart` 90 s (fixed by MSG_05). On paper a team that walks straight there now makes it, which the old speeds ruled out; in practice T_ROAD_PATROL, the compound guard and T_COMPOUND_RELIEF stand in the way. Left to Tijmen: lengthen the route or accept the sharper puzzle (see the PR).
- T_ROAD_PATROL: P_NORTH_GATE ↔ P_GENERATOR about 19 cells = 15 s each way, 60 s pause at each end: a 150 s loop.
- Target length 20 → 17 min: the walks (about 60-100 s each) and the shorter rebuilt-mast detour take about 3 min less.
