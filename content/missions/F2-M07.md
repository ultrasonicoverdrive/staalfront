## B. Story

- **Situation**: A team of analysts has decoded a set of intercepted transmissions but is trapped inside an abandoned agricultural research complex.
- **Briefing**: `briefings/F2-M07.fountain`
- **Mid-mission twist**: The complex's irrigation channels conceal a fast route for enemy scouts.
- **Story beat**: The faction obtains evidence of coordinated provocations.

- **Ending (win)**: Three analysts walk out of the orchard, and the intercept rig recorded the same forged command phrase arriving at a Compact outpost and a Directorate post in the same hour. Lenka now has the first evidence that the provocations on both sides are coordinated.
- **Ending (loss)**: An analyst dies or the rig is destroyed, and with it the only decoding of the repeating phrase. Sava withdraws what is left. The pattern Lenka suspected stays a suspicion.

## C. Map

- **Map**: new
- **Size and terrain**: Medium, 80×80 cells. An abandoned research orchard: rows of dead fruit trees (forest cells) crossed by a grid of dry concrete irrigation channels two cells wide and sunk below ground level. Greenhouse ruins in the centre, a pump house in the north-east, the analysts' shelter (a seed store) in the north-west. Player enters from the south edge along a farm track; the extraction point is the south gate where the track leaves the map.
- **Sketch**: create `missions/F2-M07-map.png` during map production.
- **Sketch description**: Draw a square walled orchard. South wall has a gate (player entry and extraction). Inside, tree rows run north–south in four blocks separated by three east–west lanes. Two long irrigation channels run north–south on the east and west sides of the orchard, each with a mouth in the north wall (enemy entry) and a mouth near the south lanes; they look like hedge lines from above. Centre: glass greenhouse ruins with a sniper tower. North-west corner: low seed store with a radio mast (the rig). North-east corner: brick pump house. North wall has two openings where the channels pass under it.

## J. Flow in words

1. 0:00 — Briefing. Player has 18 units and a truck at the south gate, no base. Objective marker on the north-west corner.
2. 0:00–3:00 — Player moves west along the south lane; OBJ_SOUTH_TOWER covers the lane and must be flanked through the trees or killed with rocket troopers. Scouts sent ahead into the tree rows are invisible to the sniper.
3. 3:00–5:00 — Entering the mid lane springs T_MID_AMBUSH out of what looked like a hedge (TR_03); if no scout has already found a channel, the ambush reveals them (TR_04). Lenka explains the channels; Sava advises gun teams at the mouths.
4. 5:00–7:00 — Player chooses: clear the greenhouses (sniper first) or bypass them through the west tree rows. Medics keep infantry alive against the sniper's picked shots.
5. 6:00 — Reinforcement truck (medic, 2 riflemen) arrives at the gate (TR_08).
6. 7:00–9:00 — Shelter picket cleared; infantry enters the seed store. Analysts join the player (TR_06). The rig stays; it cannot be moved.
7. 9:00–14:00 — The hunt: every 100 s two light transports race south through the channels (64 cells ÷ 2.5 = 26 s) and unload riflemen aimed at the analysts (max 4 waves, 10 units each — D3). At 11:30 T_RIG_RAID comes down the west channel for the rig; a heavy_gun_team or bunker at (6, 14)–(6, 20) stops it. The player splits forces: escort south, picket at the rig.
8. 10:00–13:00 — Analysts travel south (66 cells: 1:50 on foot at 0.6, 30 s in the transport_truck), preferably in the transport_truck with medics walking beside it, through the west rows and the south lane. Gun teams at the channel mouths kill transports as they emerge.
9. 13:00–16:00 — Last wave (the 4th tick is 400 s after the analysts are secured); all three analysts enter the south gate zone.
10. ~16:30 — MSG_08, win. Optional: log tape from the pump house (evidence_F2-M07).

## K. After the mission

- **Carry over**: Campaign flag `completed_F2-M07`. Evidence flag `evidence_F2-M07` if OBJ_LOG_TAPE was picked up; it adds a line to the F2-M08 briefing naming the frequencies. Surviving units do not carry over. medic becomes buildable from F2-M08.
- **Choice**: Continue to F2-M08.
- **Test scenario**:
  1. TR_01 fires once; channels are drawn as hedges and are impassable to player units.
  2. Park a scout at P_SCOUT_LOOKOUT_W for 3 s: TR_02 fires once, channels become visible and passable, MSG_03 then MSG_04.
  3. Skip scouting, walk into Z_LANE_MID: ambush emerges and 5 s later the channels are revealed by TR_04; TR_02 cannot fire again afterwards.
  4. Enter Z_SHELTER while 3 picket riflemen are alive: analysts are not transferred; kill two more: transfer occurs on the next entry.
  5. Kill OBJ_ANALYST_2 with friendly fire: immediate loss (TR_11).
  6. Destroy OBJ_INTERCEPT_RIG with the player's own rockets before TR_06: immediate loss (TR_12).
  7. Bring two analysts to the gate and leave one in the truck outside: no win; drive the truck in: TR_10 fires.
  8. Count channel waves: exactly 4 T_CHANNEL_RUNNERS waves, then none.
  9. Lose every combat unit before 6:00: no loss until the reinforcement has arrived; lose them all after: TR_13 fires.
  10. Pick up OBJ_LOG_TAPE: MSG_11, flag set, objective E completed once.

## Notes

This is an original real-time-strategy campaign inspired by the broad structure of classic 1990s RTS campaigns: faction-specific operations, base-building, commando missions, escorts, timed defence, sabotage, and escalating counterattacks. It does not reuse Red Alert characters, dialogue, mission scripts, or named story events.
